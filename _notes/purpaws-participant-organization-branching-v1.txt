PURPAWS PARTICIPANT / ORGANIZATION BRANCHING v1

STATUS
Defined for verification.
Not yet locked.


================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================

This document defines how canonical PurPaws intake branches between:

- the human participant
- an organization the participant represents
- an informal group the participant represents
- a household context
- a companion context
- an organizer / steward context
- other declared acting contexts

This document does not implement the branch.

It defines the semantic branching design that implementation must later follow.


================================================================================
GOVERNING ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

This design is governed by the locked:

1. PurPaws Canonical Network Ontology v1
2. PurPaws Canonical Onboarding Mapping v1
3. PurPaws Canonical Intake Contract v1
4. PurPaws Canonical Intake Vocabulary v1

This branching design may not redefine those layers.


================================================================================
CORE PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

A human account and an organization are not the same thing.

A participant may act:

- for themselves
- for an organization
- for an informal group
- for a household
- in relation to a companion
- as an organizer or steward
- in another declared capacity

The intake experience must determine the context in which the participant is
acting without turning that context into the participant's permanent identity.


================================================================================
THE HUMAN PARTICIPANT
================================================================================

The authenticated User represents the account holder.

The User is not automatically:

- a business
- a rescue
- a creator
- a Founder
- a Partner
- an organization
- a commercial participant
- a community-serving participant

Those meanings belong to other canonical dimensions, roles, entities,
relationships, or classifications.

A person may participate in multiple contexts over time.


================================================================================
THE ORGANIZATION
================================================================================

An organization is a canonical entity distinct from the User.

An organization may have:

- a name
- organization facts
- contact facts
- location facts
- website evidence
- activity declarations
- reviewed activity classification
- eligibility state
- commerce requirement
- commerce fulfillment state
- public projections
- relationships
- people connected to it

An organization's classification does not automatically classify the person
representing it.


================================================================================
THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN PERSON AND ORGANIZATION
================================================================================

When a participant acts for an organization, PurPaws must preserve both:

1. the person
2. the organization

and establish a relationship between them.

Conceptually:

User
    ↓
Organization Relationship
    ↓
Organization

The relationship answers questions such as:

- who represents this organization?
- what is this person's relationship to it?
- is that relationship active?
- what permissions may eventually follow from that relationship?

The organization must not be collapsed into the User record.

The User must not be collapsed into the organization record.


================================================================================
ACTING CAPACITY BRANCH
================================================================================

The locked acting_capacity vocabulary determines the intake context.

Conceptual branch:

acting_capacity
    |
    +-- self
    |
    +-- organization
    |
    +-- informal_group
    |
    +-- household
    |
    +-- companion
    |
    +-- organizer_steward
    |
    +-- other

The branch controls which additional facts intake needs.

It does not itself create a canonical role.


================================================================================
SELF BRANCH
================================================================================

When:

acting_capacity = self

PurPaws may collect person-oriented facts relevant to the chosen entry
experience.

No organization is implied.

No organization membership is implied.

No commercial classification is implied.

No Partner relationship is implied.


================================================================================
ORGANIZATION BRANCH
================================================================================

When:

acting_capacity = organization

PurPaws must be able to collect organization facts separately from the
participant's account facts.

Typical facts may include:

- organization name
- organization type or descriptive category
- website
- location
- contact information
- participant's relationship to the organization
- declared activity nature
- supporting evidence

The organization branch does not determine:

- final activity classification
- eligibility
- commerce requirement
- commerce fulfillment
- Partner status
- Founder status


================================================================================
INFORMAL GROUP BRANCH
================================================================================

An informal group must not be forced to falsely declare itself as a legally
incorporated organization.

PurPaws may preserve:

- group name
- people involved
- purpose
- location
- website or social evidence
- declared activity
- relationship to the participant

Examples may include:

- informal rescue groups
- foster networks
- volunteer groups
- neighbourhood initiatives
- community projects

Legal structure and community function remain separate facts.


================================================================================
HOUSEHOLD BRANCH
================================================================================

A household context may represent participation involving more than one person
without requiring creation of an organization.

A household is not automatically:

- an organization
- a commercial entity
- a role
- a public profile

Implementation details remain deferred.


================================================================================
COMPANION BRANCH
================================================================================

When intake concerns a companion, the human account remains the participant
account while the companion may become a distinct canonical entity.

Conceptually:

User
    ↓
relationship / stewardship
    ↓
Companion

The companion must not be represented as the User.

The User must not be represented as the companion.


================================================================================
ORGANIZER / STEWARD BRANCH
================================================================================

An organizer or steward capacity describes why the participant is entering
facts for something else.

Examples may include stewardship of:

- an event
- a memorial
- a community initiative
- another network record

Organizer / steward is an intake context.

It does not automatically become a permanent global User role.


================================================================================
ENTRY EXPERIENCE IS INDEPENDENT
================================================================================

The onboarding entry experience and acting capacity are separate dimensions.

For example:

Directory entry experience
    +
acting_capacity = organization

is possible.

But so is:

Directory entry experience
    +
acting_capacity = self

Likewise:

Partner entry experience

does not mean:

acting_capacity = commercial

and does not mean:

participant identity = Partner.

Partner remains a relationship / collaboration status.


================================================================================
CLASSIFICATION REMAINS SEPARATE
================================================================================

After the acting context is understood, activity classification remains a
separate process.

The following must remain distinct:

acting_capacity

declared_activity_nature

activity_nature

eligibility_status

commerce_requirement

commerce_status

Email verification remains separate from all six.


================================================================================
COMMERCIAL ORGANIZATIONS
================================================================================

A commercial organization is still an organization.

Commercial is not an entity type.

Commercial classification may influence:

- eligibility
- commerce requirement
- publication conditions
- package applicability

It does not change the canonical nature of the organization.


================================================================================
COMMUNITY-SERVING ORGANIZATIONS
================================================================================

A community-serving organization is still an organization.

Community-serving classification may apply to:

- registered charities
- nonprofit organizations
- informal rescues
- foster networks
- volunteer groups
- other qualifying community-serving activity

Formal nonprofit status is evidence.

It is not the only possible basis for community eligibility.


================================================================================
REVIEW-PENDING ORGANIZATIONS
================================================================================

An organization may exist canonically while its participation classification or
eligibility remains under review.

Therefore:

organization existence
    !=
eligibility approval

and:

organization existence
    !=
commerce determination

and:

organization existence
    !=
publication entitlement

A pending organization must not freeze unrelated participation by the person.


================================================================================
EXISTING IMPLEMENTATION SEAM
================================================================================

The current implementation already contains useful pieces of this future
canonical branch.

Observed existing structures include:

- User
- OnboardingRecord
- OrganizationMember
- PartnerOrganization
- PublicProfile
- RescueProfile
- FounderProfile

OnboardingRecord currently provides transitional intake storage.

OrganizationMember already demonstrates that a User-to-organization
relationship is an existing architectural concept.

PartnerOrganization currently contains organization-oriented facts and some
classification / eligibility fields.

These existing structures are implementation evidence.

They do not independently define the canonical ontology.


================================================================================
DIRECTORY MIGRATION PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

The current implementation may publish some Directory records directly from
OnboardingRecord.

That is recognized as transitional behavior.

The canonical target is:

Onboarding
    ↓
Facts + Intent
    ↓
Canonicalization
    ↓
Person / Organization / Other Canonical Records
    ↓
Relationships
    ↓
Classification
    ↓
Eligibility
    ↓
Commerce Determination
    ↓
Publication Entitlement
    ↓
Canonical Public Projection
    ↓
Directory Aggregation

Directory therefore remains downstream.

OnboardingRecord must not become the permanent identity store merely because
the current Directory can read from it.


================================================================================
ORGANIZATION RELATIONSHIP PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

OrganizationMember is evidence of an existing relationship seam.

However, this design does not yet declare that the existing
OrganizationMember schema is sufficient for every future relationship.

Before implementation, PurPaws must determine whether the existing relationship
model can faithfully represent:

- ownership
- employment
- volunteering
- authorized representation
- stewardship
- administration
- other organization relationships

No schema change is authorized by this document.


================================================================================
MULTIPLE ORGANIZATIONS
================================================================================

One User may eventually relate to multiple organizations.

One organization may eventually relate to multiple Users.

Therefore the architecture must not assume:

User == Organization

or:

one User == exactly one Organization

or:

one Organization == exactly one User


================================================================================
MULTIPLE PARTICIPATION CONTEXTS
================================================================================

A participant may simultaneously or over time be:

- an individual community member
- steward of companions
- connected to a rescue
- creator of content
- representative of an organization
- Founder
- affiliate
- event organizer
- Partner through a specific relationship

These contexts must coexist without forcing the User into one permanent
identity bucket.


================================================================================
CANONICALIZATION RESPONSIBILITY
================================================================================

Onboarding collects facts.

Canonicalization determines what durable network records should result.

Canonicalization may determine that intake describes:

- only the participant
- a participant plus organization
- a participant plus informal group
- a participant plus companion
- a participant plus event
- a participant plus memorial record
- multiple related canonical records

This determination occurs after intake facts are available.

The onboarding path alone does not make that decision.


================================================================================
EVIDENCE PRESERVATION
================================================================================

PurPaws / FCIS should preserve provenance where meaningful.

The system should be able to distinguish:

- what the participant declared
- what evidence was supplied
- what PurPaws determined
- what was later verified
- what relationship was established
- what public projection resulted

Canonicalization should strengthen evidence rather than erase it.


================================================================================
WHITE-LABEL PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

This branching architecture is independent of PurPaws-specific frontend
branding.

A future implementation may present human-friendly questions such as:

- Are you creating this for yourself?
- Are you representing an organization?
- Are you helping a rescue or community group?
- Are you creating this for your companion?
- Are you organizing or stewarding this record?

The frontend wording may change.

The canonical branching meanings beneath it must remain stable.

LOCKED PRINCIPLES

- A human account and an Organization are distinct canonical concepts.

- The authenticated User represents the account holder.

- acting_capacity describes the immediate intake context.

- acting_capacity does not itself define permanent identity, role,
  classification, eligibility, or commerce state.

- A person acting for an Organization does not become the Organization.

- An Organization does not become the User.

- Person and Organization must be preserved as distinct records connected by
  an explicit relationship.

- One User may relate to multiple Organizations.

- One Organization may relate to multiple Users.

- Organization classification does not automatically classify the Person.

- Organization existence is distinct from eligibility approval.

- Organization existence is distinct from commerce determination.

- Organization existence is distinct from publication entitlement.

- Informal groups must not be forced into false legal-organization claims.

- Household participation does not automatically imply Organization identity.

- Companion records remain distinct from the User account.

- Organizer / steward context does not automatically become a global User role.

- Entry experience and acting capacity remain separate dimensions.

- Partner remains a relationship / collaboration status and is not equivalent
  to commercial identity or acting capacity.

- Commercial remains an activity classification, not an entity type.

- OnboardingRecord remains transitional intake storage.

- Canonicalization determines what durable records and relationships result
  from intake facts.

- OrganizationMember is an existing relationship seam but is not automatically
  declared sufficient for all future canonical relationship meanings.

- Current direct Directory publication from OnboardingRecord is transitional.

- Directory remains downstream from canonical publication.

- Canonicalization must preserve meaningful evidence and provenance for FCIS.
