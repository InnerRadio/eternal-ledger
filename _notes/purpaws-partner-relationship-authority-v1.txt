1. CORE AUTHORITY

Partner is not identity.

Canonical Organization remains identity authority.

Partner Relationship Authority records an explicit qualifying
relationship between a canonical Organization and PurPaws or an
applicable network/project context.

Legacy PartnerOrganization existence is not canonical relationship
authority.


2. QUALIFYING RELATIONSHIP TYPES

- founder_partner
- sponsor_partner
- community_partner
- strategic_partner
- campaign_partner
- network_partner


3. RELATIONSHIP BASIS

relationship_type answers what kind of Partner relationship exists.

relationship_basis answers why PurPaws has authority to assert that
relationship.

Relationship basis must be explicit and evidence-backed.

No relationship basis may be inferred from:

- organization name
- website
- location
- legacy PartnerOrganization existence


4. EVIDENCE

Canonical Partner relationship authority must be traceable to
evidence or an explicitly authoritative originating system event.

Potential evidence sources include:

- Founder membership record
- sponsorship agreement
- partnership agreement
- campaign participation record
- administrator-approved relationship record
- another explicit canonical relationship source

Legacy PartnerOrganization existence alone is insufficient.


5. RELATIONSHIP STATUS

Allowed conceptual lifecycle:

- pending
- active
- inactive
- ended

Historical relationship records should be preserved.


6. TIME

Relationship authority should support:

- relationship start
- relationship end
- created timestamp
- updated timestamp

Relationships may be open-ended or time-bounded.


7. SCOPE

Relationship scope may include:

- project
- network
- campaign
- program
- event

The same Organization may hold multiple distinct Partner
relationships when relationship type or scope differs.


8. IDEMPOTENCY

Organization alone must not be the uniqueness key.

Conceptual relationship identity must distinguish at least:

- canonical Organization
- relationship type
- relationship scope

Repeated execution from the same authoritative source must not
create duplicate relationships.

Exact database uniqueness is deferred until live implementation
inspection.


9. AUTHORITY SEPARATION

IDENTITY
    !=
RELATIONSHIP
    !=
PUBLICATION
    !=
COMMERCE

An active Partner relationship does not automatically authorize
public Partner projection.


10. LEGACY

partner-1:
unresolved / legacy

partner-2:
unresolved / legacy

Neither record is classified by this design.

Future migration requires independent evidence review.


11. FCIS

Partner Relationship Authority becomes a first-class relationship
edge suitable for:

- Opportunity Graph reasoning
- sponsorship discovery
- Founder recognition
- campaign eligibility
- network discovery
- relationship history
- Partner projection

Downstream uses do not redefine relationship authority.
