ORGANIZATION ACCESS ROLE VOCABULARY v1

STATUS
LOCKED ARCHITECTURAL VOCABULARY

PURPOSE

Define launch-safe operational Organization access roles.

These roles describe operational capability.

They are not canonical relationship claims.

CORE PRINCIPLE

ROLE NAME MUST MATCH ENFORCED CAPABILITY.

A safe-sounding role must never silently receive stronger runtime authority.

CURRENT RUNTIME GAP

The current OrganizationMember model defaults to:

    role = viewer
    status = active

The current application primarily authorizes Organization capability based on:

    OrganizationMember.status == active

rather than enforcing a role-specific capability matrix.

Current runtime therefore does not safely distinguish:

    viewer
from:
    owner

for important Organization capability.

In particular, an active OrganizationMember may currently satisfy Partner /
Organization access checks and campaign access checks regardless of role.

THIS IS NOT THE TARGET GOVERNANCE MODEL.

Until role-aware enforcement is implemented and verified:

    DO NOT CREATE AN ACTIVE OrganizationMember FOR user:8 -> organization:2.

ROLE VOCABULARY

VIEWER

Purpose:

    least-privileged Organization access

May:

    view Organization workspace information
    view Organization operational summaries
    view permitted Organization records

May NOT:

    edit Organization canonical or profile content
    create or update campaigns
    publish
    invite teammates
    change access
    assign roles
    assert ownership
    perform destructive Organization operations

EDITOR

Purpose:

    Organization content editing without access administration

May:

    perform explicitly authorized Organization content edits

May NOT:

    create or update campaigns unless separately elevated
    invite teammates
    grant or revoke access
    assign roles
    assert ownership
    exercise publication authority merely because of editor status

MANAGER

Purpose:

    operational Organization management

May:

    perform permitted Organization operational writes
    create and update Organization campaigns
    manage permitted non-governance Organization operations

May NOT automatically:

    assert ownership
    invite teammates
    grant elevated access
    transfer Organization authority
    exercise publication authority outside separate publication governance

STEWARD

Purpose:

    elevated Organization access governance

May, when implementation explicitly permits:

    administer Organization operational membership
    invite teammates
    participate in access grant / revoke workflows
    manage delegated Organization authority

Steward does NOT mean:

    legal owner
    founder
    director
    employee

Steward is an operational governance role.

OWNER

Purpose:

    strongest Organization operational authority

Owner requires independent evidence appropriate to ownership or an explicit,
auditable authority-transfer process.

Owner must never be inferred from:

    first submission
    verified onboarding
    representative relationship
    email domain
    canonicalization provenance
    pre-existing active membership
    campaign participation

Owner may eventually possess the broadest Organization operational capability,
but ownership evidence and transfer governance must be separately protected.

ROLE ORDER

For access-control purposes only:

    viewer
        <
    editor
        <
    manager
        <
    steward
        <
    owner

This ordering describes increasing operational capability.

It does NOT describe real-world social importance or canonical truth.

PUBLICATION AUTHORITY

    NOT IMPLIED BY ANY ORGANIZATION ACCESS ROLE.

Publication remains separate authority.

COMMUNICATION PERMISSION

    NOT IMPLIED BY ANY ORGANIZATION ACCESS ROLE.

Communication permission remains separate authority.

COMMERCE AUTHORITY

    NOT IMPLIED BY ROLE ALONE.

Commerce remains separately governed.

LOCATION AUTHORITY

    Organization access does not automatically imply authority over every
    physical Location associated with that Organization.

Location-scoped access is deferred until explicit Location governance exists.

TEAMMATE INVITATION

Only:

    steward
    owner

may eventually receive teammate-invitation capability.

Even then, invitation capability must be explicitly implemented and governed.

ACCESS DECISION MAPPING

A GRANT decision must specify an explicit role from this vocabulary.

A canonical relationship must not choose the role automatically.

Example:

    canonical relationship:
        representative

does NOT imply:

        viewer
        editor
        manager
        steward
        owner

The Access Decision Authority must independently decide whether access is
appropriate and which role is justified.

LAUNCH SAFETY INVARIANT

DO NOT USE OrganizationMember.role AS DOCUMENTATION ONLY.

ROLE MUST BE ENFORCED.

An active viewer must never receive manager capability.

An active editor must never receive teammate-administration capability.

A manager must never silently become a steward.

A steward must never silently become an owner.

ROLE != RELATIONSHIP.

ROLE != PUBLICATION AUTHORITY.

ROLE != COMMUNICATION PERMISSION.

ROLE != OWNERSHIP EVIDENCE.

DEFER RATHER THAN GUESS.

IMPLEMENTATION SEQUENCE

1. Lock Organization Access Role Vocabulary v1.
2. Build centralized Organization Access Enforcement Authority v1.
3. Route Organization read/write/campaign checks through that authority.
4. Verify viewer cannot write.
5. Verify editor cannot manage campaigns.
6. Verify manager can manage permitted campaigns.
7. Verify manager cannot administer teammates.
8. Verify steward / owner elevation remains explicit.
9. Lock enforcement authority.
10. Implement durable Access Decision history.
11. Only then consider the first controlled GRANT.
