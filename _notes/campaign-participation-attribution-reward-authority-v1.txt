======================================================================
PURPOSE
======================================================================

This contract defines the canonical authority boundaries for:

- Campaign
- Campaign Participation
- Attribution
- Conversion
- Reward Policy
- Reward Decision
- Reward Ledger

It is based on inspection of the existing PurPaws affiliate,
campaign, organization, relationship, publication, conversion,
and commission architecture.

This contract is architecture only.

It does NOT:

- modify database schema
- migrate existing records
- replace existing campaign models
- create token economics
- alter existing commissions
- alter Affiliate IDs
- change organization authority
- change public publication
- change dashboard access
- create a new campaign implementation

======================================================================
FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

Affiliate ID identifies WHO.

Campaign Participation identifies WHERE and WHY.

Attribution establishes WHAT happened in that context.

Reward Policy determines WHAT IT EARNS.

These authorities MUST remain distinct.

Affiliate identity MUST NOT, by itself, determine:

- Campaign identity
- Campaign ownership
- Campaign management authority
- Campaign participation
- payout policy
- reward type
- reward amount
- beneficiary
- sponsor
- organizer

======================================================================
1. CAMPAIGN AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Campaign is the first-class network object representing:

WHAT IS HAPPENING?

Examples:

- fundraiser
- event
- promotion
- community initiative
- sponsorship opportunity
- rescue support campaign
- adoption campaign
- volunteer campaign
- retail promotion
- awareness campaign

A Campaign exists independently of:

- Affiliate ID
- Partner status
- organization membership
- reward type
- cash commission
- sponsor identity

A network member may create a Campaign.

Campaign creation is participant-scoped.

Organization membership is NOT required merely to create a Campaign.

CAMPAIGN CREATOR / STEWARD

The creating member is the initial Campaign steward.

Creator / steward identity is distinct from:

- beneficiary
- sponsor
- organizer
- host
- supporting partner
- participant
- affiliate
- organization owner

A Campaign creator may be:

- an individual
- an individual affiliated with an organization
- an individual with no organization affiliation

CAMPAIGN != ORGANIZATION

CAMPAIGN CREATOR != ORGANIZATION

======================================================================
2. CAMPAIGN RELATIONSHIP AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Organizations and participants may relate to a Campaign through
explicit relationships.

Possible relationship types include:

- beneficiary
- sponsor
- organizer
- host
- supporting_partner
- steward
- collaborator

These relationships do NOT automatically establish Campaign ownership.

Examples:

Campaign
    → beneficiary → Dog Rescue X

Campaign
    → sponsor → Bank A

Campaign
    → sponsor → Pet Food Company Y

Campaign
    → organizer → Member or Organization

Campaign
    → host → Venue / Event Organization

Partner is therefore best treated as:

- a relationship context
- a sponsorship context
- an organization-management context

Partner is NOT the universal Campaign owner.

Existing PartnerCampaign machinery may remain as a specialized
organization / sponsorship / commercial layer.

It MUST NOT define the universal Campaign object.

======================================================================
3. CAMPAIGN PARTICIPATION AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Campaign Participation answers:

WHO CHOSE TO PARTICIPATE IN WHICH CAMPAIGN?

Participation may include:

- user_id
- Affiliate ID
- referral code
- Campaign identity
- participation status
- joined_at

Participation is separate from Campaign creation.

Participation is separate from Campaign management.

Participation is separate from organization authority.

A member may participate in many Campaigns using the same Affiliate ID.

Example:

Affiliate ID A123

    → Campaign A
    → Campaign B
    → Campaign C
    → Campaign D

Therefore:

Affiliate ID
    = participant identity / attribution identity

Affiliate ID
    != Campaign identity

Affiliate ID
    != reward policy

Affiliate ID
    != payout rule

Existing AffiliateCampaignEnrollment is a strong basis for future
Campaign Participation semantics.

======================================================================
4. ATTRIBUTION AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Attribution answers:

WHO caused WHAT activity,
through WHICH participation,
for WHICH Campaign?

Canonical attribution MUST preserve independently:

- participant identity
- Campaign identity
- Campaign Participation identity
- attribution event identity
- conversion identity
- evidence / provenance

Attribution MUST NOT infer Campaign identity from Affiliate ID alone.

Attribution MUST NOT infer Campaign identity from referral code alone
when the same participant may participate in multiple Campaigns.

Participant attribution
!=
Campaign attribution

Both MUST be independently provable.

CURRENT INSPECTED CONDITION

Existing Enrollment:
    participant identity: present
    Campaign identity: present

Existing Click:
    participant identity: present
    Campaign identity: present

Existing Conversion:
    participant identity: present
    Campaign identity: absent

Existing Commission:
    conversion identity: present
    participant identity: present
    Campaign identity: absent

Therefore Campaign lineage currently weakens at:

Click
    ↓
Conversion

This is the first canonical attribution repair boundary.

======================================================================
5. CONVERSION AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Conversion answers:

WHAT QUALIFYING EVENT OCCURRED?

A Conversion may represent:

- signup
- purchase
- inquiry
- donation
- registration
- referral action
- campaign-defined qualifying event

A canonical Conversion MUST be able to retain or prove:

- participant
- Campaign
- Campaign Participation
- qualifying event
- target
- conversion status
- attribution evidence

target_type / target_id identify WHAT converted.

They MUST NOT be treated as a substitute for Campaign identity.

======================================================================
6. REWARD POLICY AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Reward Policy answers:

WHAT CAN AN ELIGIBLE ATTRIBUTED EVENT EARN?

Reward Policy is Campaign-specific.

The same Affiliate ID may participate in multiple Campaigns with
different reward policies.

Example:

Affiliate ID A123

Campaign A
    → fixed cash

Campaign B
    → percentage reward

Campaign C
    → PurPaws token

Campaign D
    → retailer discount

Campaign E
    → organization benefit

Campaign F
    → no reward / voluntary participation

Therefore:

Affiliate ID cannot determine reward treatment.

Campaign attribution determines which Reward Policy applies.

Reward Policy may eventually support:

- cash
- fixed amount
- percentage
- PurPaws token
- network credit
- retailer discount
- organization benefit
- campaign perk
- mixed reward
- no reward

TOKEN

PurPaws token is one possible reward / utility instrument.

Token is NOT:

- Campaign identity
- Affiliate identity
- Campaign ownership
- Campaign Participation
- attribution authority

Token economics, transferability, redemption, valuation, and issuance
rules are OUTSIDE this v1 authority contract.

======================================================================
7. REWARD DECISION AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Reward Decision answers:

DOES THIS ATTRIBUTED EVENT QUALIFY,
AND WHAT EXACTLY SHOULD IT EARN?

Reward Decision MUST consume proven:

- Campaign
- Campaign Participation
- Attribution Context
- Conversion
- Reward Policy

Reward Decision MUST NOT accept arbitrary caller-supplied value as
canonical economic truth.

A canonical Reward Decision should determine:

- reward eligibility
- reward type
- reward amount / quantity / entitlement
- currency or unit where relevant
- beneficiary / recipient where relevant
- policy source
- attribution source
- idempotency key / qualifying event identity

======================================================================
8. REWARD LEDGER AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Reward Ledger answers:

WHAT VALUE WAS ACTUALLY:

- earned
- issued
- pending
- approved
- payable
- redeemed
- directed
- reversed
- voided
- expired

The Reward Ledger may eventually project specialized reward records.

Examples:

Reward Event
    → cash
        → AffiliateCommission

Reward Event
    → PurPaws token
        → Token Ledger Entry

Reward Event
    → network credit
        → Credit Ledger Entry

Reward Event
    → retailer discount
        → Discount Entitlement

Reward Event
    → organization benefit
        → Organization Benefit Entry

Existing AffiliateCommission may survive as a cash-specific projection
of a broader Reward system.

9. COMMISSION AUTHORITY — CURRENT CLASSIFICATION
======================================================================

Current create_affiliate_commission behavior is classified as:

PRIVILEGED
YES

Available to:
- super_admin
- admin
- developer

Ordinary member economic write:
NO

Single known AffiliateCommission creation writer:
YES

Conversion verified:
NO

Affiliate derived from Conversion:
NO

Referral derived from Conversion:
NO

Campaign identified:
NO

Campaign Participation identified:
NO

Campaign payout policy consulted:
NO

Reward amount calculated:
NO

Duplicate Commission prevention:
NO

Therefore current Commission creation is:

PRIVILEGED
BUT
UNVERIFIED ECONOMIC WRITE BOUNDARY

This does NOT mean existing Commission status lifecycle is invalid.

It means Commission creation currently stores supplied economic values
without proving the full Campaign-specific attribution and reward chain.

11. IMPLEMENTATION ORDER
======================================================================

Implementation MUST proceed upstream to downstream.

PHASE 1
Conversion Attribution

Preserve / prove Campaign identity at Conversion.

PHASE 2
Campaign Participation Linkage

Ensure Conversion can be tied to the specific Campaign Participation
that produced it.

PHASE 3
Canonical Attribution Context

Establish:

participant
+
Campaign
+
participation
+
event
+
conversion
+
evidence

PHASE 4
Reward Policy

Separate reward policy from Campaign identity.

PHASE 5
Reward Decision

Calculate reward from:

proven attribution
+
Campaign-specific policy

PHASE 6
Reward Persistence

Persist the already-proven Reward Decision.

PHASE 7
Idempotency

Prevent duplicate rewards for the same qualifying event unless a
Campaign policy explicitly permits repeated rewards.

PHASE 8
Legacy Projection / Compatibility

Preserve appropriate existing:

- AffiliateCommission
- AffiliateCampaign
- AffiliateCampaignEnrollment
- PartnerCampaign

during migration or compatibility transition.

======================================================================

Commission, token, discount, credit, or organization-benefit work
must respect this authority separation.

independently.

No organization relationship may silently become Campaign ownership.

No Partner-specific implementation may redefine universal Campaign
semantics.
