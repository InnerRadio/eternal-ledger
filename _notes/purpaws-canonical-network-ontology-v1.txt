PURPAWS CANONICAL NETWORK ONTOLOGY v1

STATUS
Defined for verification.
Not yet locked.


================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================

This document defines the canonical conceptual structure of the PurPaws network.

It exists to prevent frontend pages, onboarding paths, account roles, database
models, commercial classifications, and public directory listing types from
being treated as though they are the same kind of thing.

PurPaws is a network of participants, companion animals, organizations,
stories, relationships, activities, resources, and opportunities.

The Directory is the discoverable projection of that network.

FCIS is the intelligence system that understands and strengthens the network.

The Opportunity Graph represents potential and actual value-producing
relationships across the network.


================================================================================
GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

The database is not the Directory.

The Directory is not an account role.

An onboarding path is not necessarily an entity type.

A public profile is not necessarily the canonical record.

A commercial classification is not an identity.

A participant may have multiple roles and relationships simultaneously.

The architecture must therefore distinguish:

1. Entities
2. Roles and Relationships
3. Classifications
4. Records and Storytelling
5. Projections and Experiences
6. Intelligence


================================================================================
1. ENTITIES
================================================================================

Entities are things that can exist independently in the PurPaws network.

Canonical entity concepts include:

- Person
- Organization
- Companion
- Event
- Place / Resource
- Campaign / Opportunity

An entity may participate in multiple relationships and may appear through
multiple public or private projections.

An entity should not need to become a different entity merely because its
role, eligibility, activity, or public presentation changes.


================================================================================
2. ROLES AND RELATIONSHIPS
================================================================================

Roles and relationships describe how entities participate in PurPaws or how
they relate to one another.

Current concepts include:

- Caregiver / Steward
- Creator
- Founder
- Partner
- Affiliate
- Member
- Organization Member
- Sponsor
- Referrer
- Rescue
- Foster

These concepts must not automatically be treated as mutually exclusive.

A person may be:

- a companion caregiver,
- a Founder,
- an Affiliate,
- a Creator,
- and a foster

at the same time.

An organization may participate as:

- a rescue,
- a Partner,
- a sponsor,
- a campaign participant,
- or another community role

without requiring separate canonical identities.

Rescue and Foster remain deliberately flexible concepts.

A rescue may be:

- a legally incorporated not-for-profit organization,
- an informal community organization,
- a volunteer network,
- or another animal-welfare participant.

A foster may be:

- an individual,
- a household,
- a volunteer associated with an organization,
- or part of an informal rescue network.

Their final implementation must preserve these distinctions rather than
forcing legal status, activity, identity, and role into one field.


================================================================================
3. CLASSIFICATIONS
================================================================================

Classifications describe facts or administrative determinations about an
entity or its participation.

Current canonical classification concepts include:

- Participant / Organization Type
- Declared Activity Nature
- Activity Nature
- Eligibility Status
- Eligibility Basis
- Eligibility Review Source
- Eligibility Review Reference
- Eligibility Review Notes
- Eligibility Reviewed At
- Commerce Status
- Verification Status
- Publication Status

The canonical activity and eligibility sequence is:

Declared Activity Nature
        ↓
PurPaws Review
        ↓
Activity Nature
        ↓
Eligibility
        ↓
Commerce Status

A participant's declaration is evidence, not automatically the final
classification.

PurPaws may verify a declaration using appropriate evidence such as the
participant's public website or other reliable information.

This allows community-serving rescues, fosters, volunteer groups, and similar
participants to be evaluated according to what they actually do rather than
only according to formal corporate registration.

It also allows clearly commercial activities such as ordinary grooming,
retail, veterinary commerce, manufacturing, and other for-profit activity to
be classified appropriately.

Verification may remain pending.

Pending verification must be represented in the database so that review and
follow-up can occur without requiring the founder to personally contact every
participant.


================================================================================
4. RECORDS AND STORYTELLING
================================================================================

Records preserve information, history, evidence, activity, and stories
connected to network entities.

Current concepts include:

- Memorial
- Trail
- Story
- Photo / Media
- Contribution
- Need
- Offer
- Referral
- Follow-up
- Evidence
- Note
- AI Observation

Records may connect multiple entities.

They are not necessarily independent identities.


================================================================================
TRAILS PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

Trails are a first-class storytelling record in PurPaws.

Trails are for the living.

A Trail allows any type of eligible PurPaws participant to write and share
stories about the life they are living with companion animals and within the
PurPaws community.

A Trail is not a Memorial.

A Trail is not a Memorial Contribution.

A Trail is not derived from a Memorial.

A Trail does not require a Memorial in order to exist.

The canonical distinction is:

Trails
    The stories we're still making together.

Memorials
    The stories we carry with us.

A Trail is not the companion itself.

It is an independently existing living story record authored by a PurPaws
participant.

A Trail may connect:

- a Person / Author
- a living Companion
- a Place
- an Event or Moment
- a Rescue
- a Foster
- an Organization
- Photos / Media
- Other Companions
- other relevant network entities

Examples of Trail stories may include:

- everyday life with a companion
- morning rituals
- walks
- first hikes
- recovery walks
- adoption journeys
- foster journeys
- rescue journeys
- rainy day adventures
- road trips
- outings
- discoveries
- community experiences
- pet-friendly places
- shared adventures
- meaningful moments

The author is itself a participant in the PurPaws network.

The entities connected through the story are also part of the network.

Therefore a Trail can contribute relationship evidence to FCIS and the
Opportunity Graph while remaining, first and foremost, a human story.

The public experience may simply present that meaningful story.

FCIS may additionally understand the relationships expressed by the story.

Therefore Trails serve both:

- human storytelling
- living history
- community participation
- network relationship evidence

Trails must not be reduced to a generic blog feature.

Trails must also not be implemented as an extension of Memorial
Contributions merely because the current legacy /public/trails implementation
projects Memorial Contribution records as Trails.

The existing Contribution model remains conceptually associated with
Memorial Contributions.

The existing /public/trails implementation is therefore narrower than the
canonical Trails concept defined here and must be evaluated separately before
future Trails implementation work.

No change to Contribution, Memorial, or the existing public Trails endpoint
is authorized by this ontology definition.


================================================================================
5. PROJECTIONS AND EXPERIENCES
================================================================================

A projection is a public or operational representation of underlying network
information.

Current projections and experiences include:

- Directory
- Public Profile
- Companion Profile
- Memorial Page
- Trail / Story Page
- Opportunity Profile
- Dashboard
- Workspace

A projection should not become the canonical identity merely because it is
visible to the public.


================================================================================
DIRECTORY PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

The PurPaws Directory is the discoverable projection of the whole network.

Its purpose is to list, discover, search, filter, and connect the appropriate
public representations of PurPaws participants and participant types.

The Directory is not merely:

- a business directory
- a Partner directory
- a commercial directory
- a rescue directory
- a creator directory

Those are filtered views of the larger PurPaws network.

The existing architectural rule remains valid:

- No new directory table.
- Directory is an aggregation layer.

The Directory should aggregate appropriate public-safe records from canonical
network sources.

Directory listing_type is a projection/discovery concept and must not be
assumed to be identical to account role, legal identity, organization type,
or activity nature.


================================================================================
6. INTELLIGENCE
================================================================================

FCIS means:

Founding Community Intelligence System.

FCIS treats network records as intelligence nodes and relationships as useful
context.

Its purpose includes:

- correlating evidence
- strengthening network records
- understanding relationships
- generating useful observations
- supporting follow-up
- discovering needs and offers
- identifying opportunities
- strengthening the Opportunity Graph

Evidence may include:

- participant-submitted information
- public information
- photographs
- brochures
- notes
- referrals
- interactions
- Trails
- relationships
- follow-up observations


================================================================================
OPPORTUNITY GRAPH PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

The Opportunity Graph asks:

How can these participants create value together?

It is not limited to commercial transactions.

Potential value may include:

- rescue support
- fostering
- volunteering
- storytelling
- referrals
- community participation
- sponsorship
- campaigns
- services
- education
- resource sharing
- discovery
- collaboration
- commerce where appropriate

The Opportunity Graph is therefore built from relationships across the whole
PurPaws network rather than only from Partner records.


================================================================================
PUBLIC DISCOVERY PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

This ontology preserves the existing Public Discovery Standard:

Public discovery.
Private communication.
Platform relay.
Auditable participation.

Public projections must remain privacy-safe.

Private account information, private contact routing, security information,
and other protected data must not become public merely because an entity is
discoverable through the Directory.


================================================================================
ONBOARDING PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

Onboarding should discover facts about a participant.

It should not force participants to understand the internal PurPaws database
architecture.

Conceptually:

Who / What Are You?
        ↓
What Do You Do?
        ↓
How Do You Participate?
        ↓
Community-Serving or Commercial?
        ↓
Eligibility / Review
        ↓
Account + Network Identity
        ↓
Workspace
        ↓
Publication
        ↓
Directory Projection

Existing onboarding paths may remain useful experiences.

However, an onboarding path must not automatically become the permanent
canonical identity of the participant.


================================================================================
COMMUNITY-SERVING AND COMMERCIAL PARTICIPATION
================================================================================

PurPaws distinguishes community-serving participation from commercial
participation.

Formal not-for-profit incorporation is useful evidence but is not the only
possible basis for community-serving eligibility.

Examples that may require review include:

- informal rescue groups
- independent fosters
- volunteer animal-welfare groups
- community initiatives

Clearly commercial participants may include:

- groomers
- veterinary businesses
- pet stores
- pet-food manufacturers
- other for-profit businesses

Commercial classification and access rules belong to eligibility / commerce
logic.

They must not redefine the participant's canonical identity.


================================================================================
EXISTING MODEL → CANONICAL CONCEPT MAPPING
================================================================================

The following is an architectural mapping, not a database migration.

User
    Account-backed person / identity foundation.

OrganizationMember
    Relationship between a User and an Organization.

PartnerOrganization
    Existing organization record.
    Candidate for evolution toward the canonical Organization concept.
    Do not rename or replace until dependencies are fully inspected.

PublicProfile
    Public projection/profile information.
    Not the canonical person itself.

FounderProfile
    Founder recognition / participation projection.
    Founder is primarily a role/status relationship.

RescueProfile
    Existing rescue-oriented record.
    Final canonical mapping remains open pending dependency inspection.

RescueAnimal
    Companion-related rescue record.
    Final relationship to canonical Companion remains open pending inspection.

Memorial
    Storytelling / remembrance record connected to companion identity and
    stewardship.

Trail
    First-class living-story record.
    Authored by a PurPaws participant.
    Independent of Memorials and Memorial Contributions.
    May preserve connections to a living Companion, author, place, event,
    rescue, foster, organization, media, and other relevant network entities.

PartnerCampaign
    Campaign / Opportunity-related entity.

PartnerTaxonomyCategory
    Existing classification taxonomy.

PartnerTaxonomyAssignment
    Existing relationship between organization/campaign and taxonomy.

OnboardingRecord
    Intake / transitional record used while participant identity and
    workspace information are being established.

Public Directory
    Aggregated public projection.
    Not a canonical database entity.


================================================================================
OPEN QUESTIONS
================================================================================

The following remain intentionally unresolved until inspected:

1. Whether PartnerOrganization should evolve into or be supplemented by a
   generalized Organization model.

2. Exact canonical mapping of RescueProfile.

3. Exact canonical mapping of RescueAnimal to Companion.

4. Whether Creator should remain primarily account-role based or become a
   relationship / participation record.

5. Whether Partner should remain represented through PartnerOrganization or
   become a relationship/classification attached to Organization.

6. How Founder status should eventually relate to generalized participant
   relationships.

7. How Trails should connect to canonical Companion records as the companion
   architecture evolves.

8. How existing Partner taxonomy should evolve into broader PurPaws network
   taxonomy without destroying useful existing work.

These questions are IMPLEMENTATION OPEN.

They are not blockers to Canonical Network Ontology v1 because the governing
conceptual distinctions are already defined.

They must be resolved by inspection before any structural replacement,
migration, merger, renaming, or implementation that depends upon them.


================================================================================
IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARIES
================================================================================

This ontology does NOT authorize immediate database restructuring.

Specifically, this document does not authorize:

- renaming PartnerOrganization
- deleting existing profile models
- merging tables
- replacing onboarding paths
- changing existing API contracts
- changing Directory response shapes
- changing payment or eligibility behavior
- changing taxonomy structures

Those actions require their own:

Observe
    ↓
Inspect
    ↓
Understand
    ↓
Replace / Add
    ↓
Verify
    ↓
Lock
    ↓
Continue

cycle.


================================================================================
WHITE-LABEL PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

PurPaws architecture must remain modular and capable of supporting future
white-label communities.

Canonical network concepts must therefore remain separate from:

- PurPaws-specific page layouts
- version directories
- frontend route names
- temporary onboarding experiences
- presentation-specific listing types

Shared intelligence and network architecture should be reusable without
requiring a white-label implementation to reproduce PurPaws presentation
choices.
