======================================================================
PURPOSE
======================================================================

Attribution Context is the first-class authority that preserves:

WHO REFERRED OR ORIGINATED THE ACTIVITY

FOR WHICH CAMPAIGN

THROUGH WHAT INBOUND EVIDENCE

BEFORE MEMBERSHIP EXISTS

THROUGH ACCOUNT CREATION

INTO CAMPAIGN PARTICIPATION

AND EVENTUALLY INTO CONVERSION

Attribution Context exists because:

AffiliateClick
    captures inbound evidence

AffiliateCampaignEnrollment
    captures identified Campaign participation

AffiliateConversion
    captures a qualifying event

but no existing record canonically preserves the lineage between them.

======================================================================
1. FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

Attribution Context may exist BEFORE membership.

Campaign Participation requires an identified member.

Membership resolution MUST preserve:

- original Campaign
- original referrer
- original Affiliate identity of the referrer
- original referral code
- original inbound evidence
- original attribution origin

The new member receives their OWN Affiliate identity.

Referrer identity MUST NOT become participant identity.

Participant identity MUST NOT overwrite referrer identity.

======================================================================
2. EXAMPLE LIFECYCLE
======================================================================

Member A has:

    user_id = A
    Affiliate ID = AFF-A
    referral code = REF-A

Member A shares:

    "Support Campaign X on PurPaws"

Person B clicks.

At that moment:

    B may not yet have a PurPaws account.

Therefore B CANNOT yet have:

    Campaign Enrollment
    participant user_id
    participant Affiliate ID

But Attribution Context CAN already preserve:

    Campaign X
    referring Member A
    AFF-A
    REF-A
    inbound click
    source
    destination
    timestamp
    other evidence

Then B registers.

B receives:

    user_id = B
    Affiliate ID = AFF-B
    referral code = REF-B

B may then join Campaign X.

Campaign Participation belongs to B:

    campaign_id = X
    user_id = B
    affiliate_id = AFF-B
    referral_code = REF-B

Original attribution remains:

    referrer = A
    referring affiliate = AFF-A
    referring referral code = REF-A

These identities MUST remain distinct.

======================================================================
3. ATTRIBUTION CONTEXT LIFECYCLE
======================================================================

STATE 1
ANONYMOUS / INBOUND

Known:

- Campaign may be known
- referring Affiliate may be known
- referring referral code may be known
- click or source evidence may be known

Unknown:

- future member user_id
- future participant Affiliate ID
- future Enrollment

STATE 2
IDENTIFIED

Membership has been created.

Attribution Context may now additionally know:

- resolved user_id
- account creation event
- registration target

Original referrer evidence MUST remain immutable in meaning.

STATE 3
PARTICIPANT

The identified member joins the Campaign.

Attribution Context may now additionally know:

- Campaign Enrollment / Participation identity
- participant Affiliate ID
- participant referral code

STATE 4
CONVERTED

A qualifying event occurs.

Attribution Context may now be associated with:

- Conversion
- conversion type
- conversion target
- conversion status

STATE 5
REWARD-CONSUMABLE

Once attribution is proven, downstream Reward Policy may consume it.

Attribution Context itself MUST NOT calculate reward value.

======================================================================
4. REFERRER VS PARTICIPANT
======================================================================

REFERRER

Answers:

WHO BROUGHT THIS PERSON OR ACTIVITY HERE?

May contain:

- referring_user_id
- referring_affiliate_id
- referring_referral_code

PARTICIPANT

Answers:

WHO IS NOW PARTICIPATING IN THIS CAMPAIGN?

May contain:

- participant_user_id
- participant_affiliate_id
- participant_referral_code
- enrollment_id

These authorities MUST NOT collapse.

Example:

A refers B.

A's Affiliate ID proves referral provenance.

B receives a new Affiliate ID.

B's Affiliate ID belongs to B's future participation.

A remains the historical referrer.

======================================================================
5. CAMPAIGN AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Attribution Context may reference one Campaign.

Campaign identity must come from proven inbound context.

Campaign identity MUST NOT be reconstructed later from Affiliate ID alone.

Campaign identity MUST NOT be reconstructed later merely because a
current Enrollment happens to share the same Affiliate ID or referral code.

Historical proof must originate at or before the attributed event.

======================================================================
6. PARTICIPATION AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Campaign Participation remains authoritative for:

- identified user
- Campaign membership
- participant Affiliate ID
- participant referral code
- participation status
- joined_at

Attribution Context may link to Campaign Participation.

It MUST NOT replace Campaign Participation.

Before membership:

    enrollment_id = unavailable

After the user joins the Campaign:

    enrollment_id may be resolved

Attribution Context may therefore mature over time without changing
the historical meaning of the inbound evidence.

======================================================================
7. CLICK / INBOUND EVIDENCE AUTHORITY
======================================================================

AffiliateClick remains evidence of an inbound interaction.

It may preserve:

- campaign_id
- affiliate_id
- referral_code
- ad_id
- source_url
- destination_url
- IP address
- user agent
- created_at

AffiliateClick does NOT prove:

- authenticated user identity
- Campaign Enrollment
- Conversion
- Reward eligibility

Attribution Context may reference AffiliateClick as evidence.

Click is optional evidence.

Not every legitimate attribution requires a browser click.

======================================================================
8. NON-CLICK ATTRIBUTION
======================================================================

Valid future attribution evidence may include:

- Campaign share link
- QR code
- event check-in
- referral code
- invitation
- admin-verified referral
- in-person Campaign registration
- partner-originated referral
- other explicit provenance

Therefore Attribution Context MUST NOT require click_id universally.

Evidence source should remain extensible.

======================================================================
9. CONTEXT HANDOFF
======================================================================

Current inspected architecture captures raw Campaign/referral context
but does not carry it durably into registration.

A future handoff mechanism may use:

- signed attribution token
- attribution context ID
- cookie
- browser storage
- query parameter
- registration payload field
- authenticated session reference

These are TRANSPORT mechanisms.

They are NOT canonical attribution truth.

The authoritative record is Attribution Context.

Transport mechanisms may point to or carry an Attribution Context
reference.

======================================================================
10. REGISTRATION CONTRACT
======================================================================

Registration MUST NOT infer Campaign from Affiliate ID alone.

Registration may receive a valid Attribution Context reference.

Registration should verify that context before resolving it to the
newly created user.

On successful account creation:

- original referrer remains preserved
- resolved user_id may be added
- participant Affiliate ID is newly issued to the new user
- participant Affiliate ID MUST NOT overwrite referring Affiliate ID

The registration experience may remain continuous for the user:

    Join Campaign X
        ↓
    Create free PurPaws account
        ↓
    Account created
        ↓
    Campaign participation completed

The authority model behind that experience remains separated.

======================================================================
11. CAMPAIGN PARTICIPATION AFTER REGISTRATION
======================================================================

If the inbound action expresses intent to join Campaign X:

after account creation the system may create or complete:

    AffiliateCampaignEnrollment

for the NEW member.

Enrollment should use:

- Campaign X
- new user_id
- new member Affiliate ID
- new member referral code

Attribution Context then links the original inbound provenance
to the resulting Participation.

======================================================================
12. CONVERSION CONTRACT
======================================================================

AffiliateConversion currently loses Campaign / Participation context.

Future Conversion attribution MUST be able to prove:

- which Attribution Context produced it
- which Campaign applies
- which Campaign Participation applies where relevant
- which participant applies
- which evidence supports the attribution

Conversion MUST NOT independently reconstruct Campaign lineage.

Conversion should consume already-proven attribution.

======================================================================
13. REWARD BOUNDARY
======================================================================

Attribution Context establishes provenance.

Reward Policy establishes treatment.

Reward Decision establishes value.

Therefore:

Attribution Context
    != Reward Policy

Attribution Context
    != Reward Amount

Attribution Context
    != Commission

Attribution Context must remain reward-neutral.

The same attribution authority must be usable for:

- cash commission
- percentage reward
- PurPaws token
- network credit
- retailer discount
- organization benefit
- campaign perk
- no reward

======================================================================
14. MUTABILITY / RESOLUTION
======================================================================

Attribution Context may gain resolved identities over time:

anonymous
    ↓
identified
    ↓
participant
    ↓
converted

However, historical provenance fields must not silently change meaning.

Examples of historical provenance:

- original Campaign
- original referrer
- original referring Affiliate ID
- original referring referral code
- original evidence source
- original click reference
- original inbound timestamp

Resolution may ADD later identity.

Resolution must not rewrite historical origin.

======================================================================
15. EXPIRY / VALIDITY
======================================================================

Attribution Context may eventually require:

- expiry rules
- validity window
- invalidation state
- supersession rules
- conflict resolution
- source confidence

These rules are NOT defined in this v1 contract.

They require later inspection and policy design.

======================================================================
16. CURRENT EXISTING RECORDS
======================================================================

AffiliateClick

Role:
Inbound evidence

Preserve.

AffiliateCampaignEnrollment

Role:
Campaign Participation

Preserve.

AffiliateConversion

Role:
Qualifying event

Preserve, but future attribution linkage is required.

AffiliateCommission

Role:
Cash-specific downstream economic record

Preserve.

None of these records should be overloaded to become the entire
Attribution Context authority.

======================================================================
17. REQUIRED FIRST-CLASS AUTHORITY
======================================================================

A first-class Attribution Context record is justified because no
existing inspected model can represent all of:

- pre-membership origin
- Campaign identity
- referrer identity
- inbound evidence
- later resolved member identity
- later Campaign Participation identity
- subsequent Conversion lineage

without changing the meaning of that existing model.

======================================================================
19. HARD INVARIANTS
======================================================================

1.
Attribution Context may precede membership.

2.
Campaign Participation requires an identified member.

3.
Referrer identity != participant identity.

4.
Referring Affiliate ID != participant Affiliate ID.

5.
Original Campaign provenance must survive membership resolution.

6.
Original referrer provenance must survive membership resolution.

7.
Click evidence != Campaign Participation.

8.
Campaign Participation != Attribution Context.

9.
Conversion != Attribution Context.

10.
Attribution Context != Reward Policy.

11.
Attribution Context must remain reward-neutral.

12.
Campaign must not be reconstructed from Affiliate ID alone.

13.
Current Enrollment state must not be used as retroactive historical
proof for an earlier Conversion.

14.
Context must be captured before or during the attributed journey,
not manufactured after the fact.

15.
Transport mechanism != attribution authority.

======================================================================
20. USER EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

The authority architecture may be complex internally.

The user journey should remain simple.

Example:

    Click "Support Campaign X"
        ↓
    Campaign page
        ↓
    "Join this Campaign"
        ↓
    Create free PurPaws account
        ↓
    Account created
        ↓
    Campaign joined
        ↓
    Continue supporting Campaign X

The system must preserve provenance without forcing the user to
manually rediscover the Campaign after registration.
