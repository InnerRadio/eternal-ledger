PURPAWS CANONICAL INTAKE VOCABULARY v1

STATUS
Defined for verification.
Not yet locked.


================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================

This document defines the semantic vocabulary used by the PurPaws Canonical
Intake Contract.

It is governed by:

- PurPaws Canonical Network Ontology v1
- PurPaws Canonical Onboarding Mapping v1
- PurPaws Canonical Intake Contract v1

All three governing documents are locked.

This vocabulary exists to prevent different semantic concepts from being
collapsed into overloaded status fields or frontend path labels.


================================================================================
GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

PurPaws must preserve the difference between:

1. how a person is acting during intake
2. what the participant declares about an activity
3. what PurPaws determines about that activity
4. whether the relevant participation is eligible
5. whether a commercial condition applies
6. the fulfillment state of that commercial condition

These are six separate semantic dimensions:

acting_capacity

declared_activity_nature

activity_nature

eligibility_status

commerce_requirement

commerce_status

They must not be silently substituted for one another.


================================================================================
1. ACTING CAPACITY
================================================================================

FIELD

acting_capacity


QUESTION ANSWERED

On whose behalf, or in what immediate capacity, is this person completing
this intake?


SEMANTIC TYPE

Intake context.


NOT

acting_capacity is NOT:

- permanent participant identity
- canonical entity type
- participant role
- relationship status
- activity classification
- eligibility status
- commerce requirement
- commerce status
- Directory type


CANONICAL CONCEPTUAL VALUES

self

    The person is acting for themselves.

organization

    The person is acting for an organization, institution, business,
    association, rescue, or other organized entity.

informal_group

    The person is acting for a group that may not have a formal legal or
    organizational structure.

household

    The person is acting in a household / family context.

companion

    The person is acting in relation to a companion animal.

organizer_steward

    The person is acting as the immediate organizer or steward of the thing
    being created or submitted.

other

    The intake does not fit an established acting-capacity value and requires
    additional facts or future classification.


================================================================================
ACTING CAPACITY IS NOT ROLE
================================================================================

The following concepts must NOT automatically become acting_capacity values:

- caregiver
- steward
- owner
- family member
- foster
- organization member
- organization administrator
- organizer
- creator
- founder
- partner
- sponsor
- collaborator
- author

These are candidate canonical roles or relationships.

Example:

acting_capacity = organization

may later produce:

Organization
        +
OrganizationMember relationship
        +
administrator role

Another example:

acting_capacity = companion

may later produce:

Companion
        +
caregiver / steward relationship

The intake context and resulting canonical relationship must remain distinct.


================================================================================
ACTING CAPACITY IS NOT COMMERCIAL STATUS
================================================================================

The following must never be acting_capacity values merely because they
describe the participant or activity:

- commercial
- community_serving
- mixed
- verified_commercial
- verified_community
- partner
- founder

A person acting for a commercial Organization still has:

acting_capacity = organization

The Organization's activity and eligibility are classified separately.


================================================================================
2. DECLARED ACTIVITY NATURE
================================================================================

FIELD

declared_activity_nature


QUESTION ANSWERED

How does the participant describe the nature of the relevant activity?


SEMANTIC TYPE

Participant declaration / evidence.


CANONICAL CONCEPTUAL VALUES

community_serving

    The participant declares that the relevant activity primarily serves a
    community, welfare, rescue, foster, charitable, volunteer, public-benefit,
    or similar non-commercial purpose.

commercial

    The participant declares that the relevant activity primarily involves
    commercial products, services, sales, paid business activity, or another
    commercial purpose.

mixed

    The participant declares that the relevant activity contains meaningful
    community-serving and commercial characteristics.

uncertain

    The participant cannot reasonably determine which declaration best
    describes the activity.


================================================================================
DECLARATION PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

declared_activity_nature records what the participant told PurPaws.

It is evidence.

It is not automatically PurPaws' final classification.

It must not be overwritten merely because PurPaws later reaches a different
determination.

Example:

declared_activity_nature = community_serving

may coexist with:

activity_nature = commercial

if review evidence supports that determination.

Preserving both values protects the evidence history.


================================================================================
DECLARATION IS NOT LEGAL STATUS
================================================================================

declared_activity_nature must not force participants to claim a legal status
they do not possess.

For example:

community_serving

does not necessarily mean:

- registered charity
- incorporated not-for-profit
- legally registered rescue

An informal rescue, foster network, volunteer initiative, or individual
community-serving activity may truthfully declare community_serving without
claiming formal legal status.


================================================================================
3. ACTIVITY NATURE
================================================================================

FIELD

activity_nature


QUESTION ANSWERED

After review, how does PurPaws classify the relevant activity?


SEMANTIC TYPE

Reviewed PurPaws classification.


CANONICAL CONCEPTUAL VALUES

unclassified

    PurPaws has not yet made an activity classification.

community_serving

    PurPaws determines that the relevant activity qualifies as
    community-serving for the applicable PurPaws purpose.

commercial

    PurPaws determines that the relevant activity is commercial for the
    applicable PurPaws purpose.

mixed

    PurPaws determines that meaningful community-serving and commercial
    characteristics coexist and that the activity requires the corresponding
    mixed-treatment policy.

uncertain

    Available evidence is insufficient to make a reliable classification.


================================================================================
ACTIVITY CLASSIFICATION PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

activity_nature is a PurPaws determination.

It must remain separate from:

declared_activity_nature

The normal evidence relationship is:

Participant Declaration
        ↓
declared_activity_nature
        ↓
Evidence / Review
        ↓
activity_nature

The values may agree.

The values may differ.

A difference is not itself an error.


================================================================================
UNCLASSIFIED VS UNCERTAIN
================================================================================

unclassified means:

PurPaws has not yet performed or completed the relevant classification.

uncertain means:

PurPaws has reviewed the available evidence but cannot yet make a reliable
community-serving / commercial / mixed determination.

These states must not be treated as synonyms.


================================================================================
4. ELIGIBILITY STATUS
================================================================================

FIELD

eligibility_status


QUESTION ANSWERED

What is the current PurPaws participation-review state for the relevant
participant, entity, relationship, or activity?


SEMANTIC TYPE

Participation eligibility state.


CANONICAL CONCEPTUAL VALUES

unclassified

    No eligibility determination has yet been initiated or established.

review_pending

    Eligibility review is expected or underway and has not yet reached a
    final determination.

verified_community

    PurPaws has verified the applicable participation as eligible under
    community-serving conditions.

verified_commercial

    PurPaws has verified the applicable participation as eligible under
    commercial conditions.

review_required

    Existing information is insufficient, conflicting, exceptional, or
    otherwise requires additional human or system review before eligibility
    can be finalized.


================================================================================
ELIGIBILITY IS NOT ACTIVITY NATURE
================================================================================

activity_nature answers:

What kind of activity is this?

eligibility_status answers:

What is the state / result of PurPaws participation review?

Therefore:

activity_nature = commercial

does not itself mean:

eligibility_status = verified_commercial

A review may still be pending.


================================================================================
REVIEW_PENDING VS REVIEW_REQUIRED
================================================================================

review_pending means:

The ordinary eligibility determination has not yet finished.

review_required means:

The case requires additional review beyond an ordinary unresolved state.

They must not be treated as synonyms.


================================================================================
ELIGIBILITY SCOPE
================================================================================

eligibility_status applies to the relevant canonical subject.

It must not automatically become a global User-account status.

For example:

User
    active

Companion
    publishable under its applicable rules

Trails participation
    available under its applicable rules

Organization
    eligibility_status = review_pending

may all coexist.

An unresolved Organization review must not unnecessarily freeze unrelated
participation by the person representing it.


================================================================================
ELIGIBILITY IS NOT REJECTION
================================================================================

Neither:

unclassified

nor:

review_pending

nor:

review_required

means that the participant has been rejected.

A future rejection / ineligible state must not be invented implicitly.

If PurPaws requires such a state, it must be deliberately defined and
verified before implementation.


================================================================================
5. COMMERCE REQUIREMENT
================================================================================

FIELD

commerce_requirement


QUESTION ANSWERED

Does a commercial condition apply to the relevant participation entitlement?


SEMANTIC TYPE

Commerce-requirement determination.


CANONICAL CONCEPTUAL VALUES

unclassified

    PurPaws has not yet determined whether a commercial condition applies.

not_required

    No commercial package, payment condition, or other commercial fulfillment
    requirement applies to the relevant entitlement.

required

    A commercial package, payment condition, or other commercial fulfillment
    requirement applies to the relevant entitlement.


================================================================================
COMMERCE REQUIREMENT IS A DETERMINATION
================================================================================

commerce_requirement records whether a commercial condition applies.

It does not describe whether that condition has been fulfilled.

Therefore:

commerce_requirement = required

may coexist with any applicable commerce fulfillment state such as:

commerce_status = unstarted

commerce_status = pending

commerce_status = complete

The requirement should remain historically understandable even after the
commercial condition has been satisfied.


================================================================================
COMMERCE REQUIREMENT IS NOT IDENTITY
================================================================================

commerce_requirement must not be used as:

- participant identity
- organization identity
- Partner status
- activity nature
- eligibility status
- account verification status

A commercial Organization may or may not be a Partner.

A Partner may or may not be commercial.


================================================================================
6. COMMERCE STATUS
================================================================================

FIELD

commerce_status


QUESTION ANSWERED

What is the current fulfillment state of the applicable commercial condition?


SEMANTIC TYPE

Commerce-fulfillment state.


CANONICAL CONCEPTUAL VALUES

not_applicable

    No commerce fulfillment workflow applies because the relevant commercial
    requirement has been determined to be not required.

unstarted

    A commercial requirement applies, but its fulfillment workflow has not
    yet begun.

pending

    Fulfillment of the applicable commercial condition has begun or remains
    incomplete.

complete

    The applicable commercial condition has been completed.


================================================================================
COMMERCE STATUS DEPENDS ON COMMERCE REQUIREMENT
================================================================================

The normal relationship is:

commerce_requirement = not_required
        ↓
commerce_status = not_applicable


or:

commerce_requirement = required
        ↓
commerce_status = unstarted
        ↓
commerce_status = pending
        ↓
commerce_status = complete

commerce_requirement and commerce_status must remain separate.

The requirement describes whether the obligation exists.

The status describes the fulfillment state of that obligation.


================================================================================
UNSTARTED VS PENDING
================================================================================

unstarted means:

PurPaws has determined that a commercial requirement applies, but no
fulfillment workflow has yet begun.

pending means:

The applicable commercial fulfillment workflow has begun or otherwise remains
incomplete.

They must not be treated as synonyms.


================================================================================
COMPLETE
================================================================================

complete means:

The commercial condition required for the applicable entitlement has been
satisfied.

It does not necessarily mean:

- permanent lifetime payment
- permanent organization eligibility
- Partner status
- Founder status
- account verification
- all future commercial obligations are satisfied

The scope and duration of commercial completion belong to the applicable
commercial agreement / entitlement.


================================================================================
COMMERCE REQUIREMENT / STATUS SCOPE
================================================================================

Both commerce_requirement and commerce_status apply to the relevant
participation entitlement.

They must not automatically become global User-account states.

A person may therefore have unrelated PurPaws participation while an
Organization they represent has:

commerce_requirement = required

and:

commerce_status = pending

without globally disabling that person's unrelated account activity.


================================================================================
6. EMAIL VERIFICATION — EXISTING SEPARATE SYSTEM
================================================================================

Existing:

verification_status

remains conceptually associated with email / account verification.

It is deliberately NOT part of the six canonical intake vocabulary dimensions.

The distinction is:

verification_status
        =
Does this participant control the submitted email / account identity?

eligibility_status
        =
What is the participation-review state?

commerce_requirement
        =
Does a commercial condition apply?

commerce_status
        =
What is the fulfillment state of that commercial condition?

These systems must remain separate.


================================================================================
7. CANONICAL STATE RELATIONSHIPS
================================================================================

The following examples illustrate valid combinations.


EXAMPLE A — COMMUNITY-SERVING ORGANIZATION

acting_capacity = organization

declared_activity_nature = community_serving

activity_nature = community_serving

eligibility_status = verified_community

commerce_requirement = not_required

commerce_status = not_applicable


EXAMPLE B — COMMERCIAL ORGANIZATION

acting_capacity = organization

declared_activity_nature = commercial

activity_nature = commercial

eligibility_status = verified_commercial

commerce_requirement = required

commerce_status = unstarted

then:

commerce_status = pending

then:

commerce_status = complete


EXAMPLE C — DECLARATION REQUIRES REVIEW

acting_capacity = organization

declared_activity_nature = community_serving

activity_nature = uncertain

eligibility_status = review_required

commerce_requirement = unclassified

commerce_status = not_applicable


EXAMPLE D — ORDINARY REVIEW PENDING

acting_capacity = organization

declared_activity_nature = uncertain

activity_nature = unclassified

eligibility_status = review_pending

commerce_requirement = unclassified

commerce_status = not_applicable


EXAMPLE E — PERSON WITH UNRELATED PENDING ORGANIZATION

Person / User:
    account active
    email verified

Companion:
    governed by Companion publication rules

Trails:
    governed by Trails participation rules

Organization:
    activity_nature = unclassified
    eligibility_status = review_pending
    commerce_requirement = unclassified
    commerce_status = not_applicable

The pending Organization does not globally classify or disable the Person.

8. CANONICAL TRANSITION PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

These are semantic transition principles.

They are not yet database constraints or executable state machines.


DECLARED ACTIVITY

Participant supplies:

declared_activity_nature

The original declaration should remain historically preservable if later
changed or reviewed.


ACTIVITY NATURE

Typical conceptual progression:

unclassified
        ↓
community_serving

or:

unclassified
        ↓
commercial

or:

unclassified
        ↓
mixed

or:

unclassified
        ↓
uncertain

An uncertain classification may later become:

community_serving
commercial
mixed


ELIGIBILITY

Typical conceptual progression:

unclassified
        ↓
review_pending
        ↓
verified_community

or:

unclassified
        ↓
review_pending
        ↓
verified_commercial

A case needing additional examination may progress:

review_pending
        ↓
review_required
        ↓
review_pending
        ↓
verified_community / verified_commercial


COMMERCE REQUIREMENT

Typical community-serving determination:

unclassified
        ↓
not_required

Typical commercial determination:

unclassified
        ↓
required


COMMERCE STATUS

Where commerce_requirement = not_required:

not_applicable

Where commerce_requirement = required:

unstarted
        ↓
pending
        ↓
complete

Exact executable transition rules require separate implementation
verification.

================================================================================
11. EVIDENCE PRESERVATION
================================================================================

The canonical vocabulary must preserve the difference between:

what was declared

and:

what was determined.

This is important to FCIS.

Evidence should strengthen the intelligence record without silently rewriting
the historical claim that produced it.

Conceptually:

Declaration
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Observation
        ↓
Assessment
        ↓
Classification
        ↓
Eligibility
        ↓
Commerce Requirement
        ↓
Commerce Fulfillment
        ↓
Entitlement

Future implementation should preserve sufficient provenance to understand how
a determination was reached.


================================================================================
12. FRONTEND LANGUAGE PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

Canonical vocabulary is system language.

Frontend language may be warmer, clearer, and more human.

For example, the frontend does not necessarily need to ask:

"What is your declared_activity_nature?"

It may instead ask a clear question such as:

"Which best describes what you do?"

Likewise:

acting_capacity = organization

may be represented to the participant as:

"I'm here for an organization or group."

Canonical vocabulary must remain stable even when frontend wording evolves.


================================================================================
13. WHITE-LABEL PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

These semantic concepts must remain independent of PurPaws-specific frontend
branding.

A future white-label deployment may use different:

- labels
- onboarding cards
- descriptions
- visual design
- participant-facing terminology

while retaining the same canonical meanings beneath them.

14. IMPLEMENTATION STATUS
================================================================================

The values defined here are proposed canonical implementation vocabulary.

This version now separates commercial requirement from commercial fulfillment:

commerce_requirement
        =
whether a commercial condition applies

commerce_status
        =
the fulfillment state of that applicable condition

They are NOT yet executable enums.

They are NOT yet database constraints.

They are NOT yet API validation rules.

They are NOT yet frontend option values.

They are NOT yet state-machine rules.

Before implementation, this vocabulary must be verified against:

- the locked Canonical Network Ontology v1
- the locked Canonical Onboarding Mapping v1
- the locked Canonical Intake Contract v1
- existing backend model defaults
- existing database values
- existing API behavior
- existing frontend behavior

Any implementation conflict must be inspected before this vocabulary is
locked.

LOCKED SEMANTIC DIMENSIONS

1. acting_capacity

   Describes the immediate intake context in which the person is acting.

   It is not participant identity, role, relationship, activity
   classification, eligibility, or commerce state.


2. declared_activity_nature

   Records what the participant declares about the relevant activity.

   The declaration is evidence and must remain distinguishable from the later
   PurPaws determination.


3. activity_nature

   Records the reviewed PurPaws classification of the relevant activity.

   It must remain distinct from declared_activity_nature.


4. eligibility_status

   Records the state or result of PurPaws participation review for the
   applicable participant, entity, relationship, or activity.

   It must not automatically become a global User-account status.


5. commerce_requirement

   Records whether a commercial condition applies to the applicable
   participation entitlement.

   Canonical conceptual states:

   - unclassified
   - not_required
   - required


6. commerce_status

   Records the fulfillment state of an applicable commercial condition.

   Canonical conceptual states:

   - not_applicable
   - unstarted
   - pending
   - complete


EMAIL VERIFICATION REMAINS SEPARATE

verification_status remains conceptually associated with email / account
verification and is not one of the six canonical intake dimensions.

commerce_requirement answers:

Does a commercial obligation apply?

commerce_status answers:

commerce_requirement = required

unstarted
    ↓
pending
    ↓
complete

Likewise:

commerce_requirement = not_required

normally corresponds to:

commerce_status = not_applicable
