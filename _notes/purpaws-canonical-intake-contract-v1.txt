PURPAWS CANONICAL INTAKE CONTRACT v1

STATUS
Defined for verification.
Not yet locked.


================================================================================
PURPOSE
================================================================================

This document defines the canonical facts PurPaws onboarding is allowed and
expected to collect before those facts are translated into canonical entities,
records, roles, relationships, classifications, workspaces, and public
projections.

This document is governed by:

- PurPaws Canonical Network Ontology v1
- PurPaws Canonical Onboarding Mapping v1

Both governing documents are locked.

This contract does not redesign the user interface.

It defines the semantic contract beneath the interface.


================================================================================
GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

Onboarding collects facts and intent.

Onboarding does not directly define final ontology.

The participant tells PurPaws:

- who they are
- what they are doing
- what they are connected to
- what they are trying to create
- what they declare about their activity

PurPaws then determines:

- canonical entities
- canonical records
- relationships
- roles
- classifications
- eligibility
- commerce conditions
- public projection eligibility

A user declaration is not automatically a final PurPaws determination.


================================================================================
CANONICAL INTAKE LAYERS
================================================================================

The intake contract is organized into distinct semantic layers:

1. Identity

2. Acting Capacity

3. Intent

4. Entity / Record Facts

5. Relationship Facts

6. Activity Declaration

7. Evidence

8. Email Verification

9. Canonicalization

10. Eligibility Verification

11. Commerce Determination

12. Workspace Entitlement

13. Publication Entitlement

14. Directory Projection


================================================================================
1. IDENTITY
================================================================================

Identity answers:

Who is the human participant completing this onboarding?

Canonical starter facts include:

- display_name
- email

Later implementation may include additional identity-safe fields where
required, but the canonical starter identity must remain minimal.

Identity should not unnecessarily ask for organization details before it is
known that an organization is involved.

Identity should not infer commercial status.

Identity should not infer role.

Identity should not infer Directory type.


================================================================================
2. ACTING CAPACITY
================================================================================

Acting Capacity answers:

In what capacity is this person completing this onboarding?

Possible conceptual answers may include:

- acting for myself
- acting for an organization
- acting for an informal group
- acting for a household
- acting for a companion
- acting as an organizer / steward
- other future supported capacity

This is not itself a permanent identity type.

Its purpose is to determine what additional canonical facts are needed.


================================================================================
PERSON VS ORGANIZATION BRANCHING
================================================================================

The intake contract must explicitly separate:

PERSON
        ↓
human participant

from:

ORGANIZATION
        ↓
organization / group / institution / business / community entity

A person may represent one or more organizations.

An organization may have one or more people connected to it.

The person must not become commercially classified merely because one
organization they represent is commercial.

The organization must not become a person role merely because one person
created it.


================================================================================
3. INTENT
================================================================================

Intent answers:

What is the participant trying to do right now?

Intent may originate from a friendly frontend entry experience such as:

- Add a Companion
- Create a Memorial
- Creator Profile
- Event
- Partner Application
- Founder
- future Organization / Business intake
- future Rescue / Foster intake
- future Trails contribution

Intent is a routing fact.

Intent is not automatically:

- an entity type
- a role
- a classification
- a commerce state
- a Directory listing type


================================================================================
4. ENTITY / RECORD FACTS
================================================================================

Entity / Record Facts describe the thing the participant is creating,
representing, or connecting to.

Examples may include:

For a Companion:

- companion_name
- species
- relevant profile starter facts

For an Organization:

- organization_name
- organization_type
- location
- website_url

For an Event:

- event_name
- event_type
- location
- date / schedule information

For a Memorial:

- companion identity reference where applicable
- memorial-specific starter facts

For a Creator:

- person identity
- creator profile starter facts

These facts belong to the future canonical entity or record.

They should not become permanent merely because they were first collected in
OnboardingRecord.payload_json.


================================================================================
5. RELATIONSHIP FACTS
================================================================================

Relationship Facts answer:

How is the participant related to the entity, record, or activity?

Examples may include:

- caregiver
- steward
- owner
- family member
- foster
- rescue participant
- organization member
- organization administrator
- organizer
- creator
- founder
- partner
- sponsor
- collaborator
- author

Relationship facts must remain distinct from canonical entity identity.

Partner is a relationship / collaboration status.

Founder is primarily a role / recognition status.

Creator is primarily a participation role / status.

Foster may be a person relationship rather than an organization identity.


================================================================================
6. ACTIVITY DECLARATION
================================================================================

Activity Declaration answers:

How does the participant describe the nature of the activity they or their
organization are performing?

The canonical intake field is:

declared_activity_nature

This is a participant declaration.

It is not automatically the final PurPaws classification.

Potential declared values may include conceptual categories such as:

- community_serving
- commercial
- mixed
- uncertain

Exact implementation vocabulary must be verified before coding.

The participant should not be forced to understand legal or accounting
terminology that is unnecessary to PurPaws classification.


================================================================================
FORMAL NOT-FOR-PROFIT STATUS
================================================================================

Formal not-for-profit registration may be useful evidence.

It must not be treated as the only way to qualify as community-serving.

PurPaws may encounter:

- registered charities
- registered not-for-profits
- informal rescues
- foster networks
- volunteer animal-welfare groups
- community initiatives
- individuals performing community-serving activity

The intake contract must preserve enough information to review these cases
without forcing them into false legal categories.


================================================================================
COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY
================================================================================

Commercial activity is not participant identity.

Examples may include:

- groomers
- veterinarians
- pet stores
- pet food manufacturers
- commercial service businesses
- commercial product businesses

A commercial participant may still be a valid PurPaws participant.

Commercial classification may determine that a package or payment condition
must be completed before the applicable publication or participation
entitlement is enabled.

Commercial does not automatically mean Partner.

Partner does not automatically mean commercial.


================================================================================
7. EVIDENCE
================================================================================

Evidence supports later PurPaws review.

The simplest evidence may be:

- website_url

Additional evidence may later include:

- public social profile
- public organization page
- registration reference
- public event page
- other appropriate public reference

Evidence is not necessarily proof by itself.

It is material PurPaws may use when reviewing classification and eligibility.


================================================================================
WEBSITE REVIEW PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

Where appropriate, PurPaws may review a participant or organization's public
website instead of requiring manual telephone follow-up.

A website review may help establish:

- what the organization actually does
- whether activity appears community-serving
- whether activity appears commercial
- whether stated rescue / foster activity is credible
- whether additional review is required

The review result must be recorded separately from the participant's original
declaration.


================================================================================
8. EMAIL VERIFICATION
================================================================================

Email Verification answers:

Does the participant control the submitted email address?

Existing onboarding verification_status currently serves this purpose.

Conceptual states include:

- pending
- verified

Email verification may enable:

- account completion
- password setup
- account access
- dashboard / workspace access

Email verification does NOT determine:

- community eligibility
- commercial classification
- package requirement
- organization legitimacy
- final publication entitlement


================================================================================
EMAIL VERIFICATION IS NOT ELIGIBILITY VERIFICATION
================================================================================

This distinction is mandatory.

verification_status
        =
email / account verification

eligibility_status
        =
PurPaws participation review

commerce_status
        =
commercial condition / completion state

These fields must not be collapsed into one status.


================================================================================
9. CANONICALIZATION
================================================================================

Canonicalization occurs after sufficient facts are available.

Canonicalization answers:

What actual PurPaws entities, records, roles, and relationships do these facts
represent?

Examples:

Companion intent
        ↓
Companion entity
        +
caregiver / steward relationship

Creator intent
        ↓
Person participant
        +
Creator participation / role
        +
public profile projection

Organization intake
        ↓
Organization entity
        +
OrganizationMember relationship

Partner intent
        ↓
participant / organization
        +
Partner relationship where applicable

Memorial intent
        ↓
Memorial record
        +
steward / author relationship

Canonicalization must not be inferred solely from the frontend path name.


================================================================================
10. ELIGIBILITY VERIFICATION
================================================================================

Eligibility Verification answers:

Under what participation conditions may this participant, organization, or
activity proceed?

Eligibility is not identity.

Eligibility is not email verification.

Eligibility is not payment completion.

Canonical eligibility states should support at least these conceptual
conditions:

- unclassified
- review_pending
- verified_community
- verified_commercial
- review_required

Exact implementation vocabulary must be independently verified before coding.


================================================================================
ELIGIBILITY REVIEW PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

The participant may declare their activity nature.

PurPaws makes the final eligibility determination.

Canonical sequence:

Declared Activity Nature
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
PurPaws Review
        ↓
Activity Nature
        ↓
Eligibility Status
        ↓
Commerce Status


================================================================================
REVIEW PENDING
================================================================================

review_pending means:

PurPaws has not yet finalized the participation conditions that depend upon
eligibility.

review_pending is not rejection.

review_pending is not commercial.

review_pending is not community approval.

review_pending is not email verification failure.


================================================================================
ELIGIBILITY SCOPE PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

Eligibility must apply to the specific participant, entity, relationship, or
activity whose participation terms are being reviewed.

Eligibility review must not unnecessarily freeze the person's entire PurPaws
account.

For example:

A person may have:

- an active User account
- a Companion
- Trails participation
- other ordinary community activity

while an Organization they represent remains:

- eligibility_status = review_pending

The Organization's publication or commercial entitlement may remain pending
without disabling unrelated participant activity.


================================================================================
11. COMMERCE DETERMINATION
================================================================================

Commerce Determination answers:

What commercial condition, if any, applies after eligibility has been
determined?

Commerce status is not identity.

Commerce status is not Partner status.

Commerce status is not email verification.

Conceptual commerce states should support conditions such as:

- unclassified
- not_required
- required
- pending
- complete

Exact implementation vocabulary must be independently verified before coding.


================================================================================
COMMERCIAL ROUTING PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

A participant should not first be admitted under an apparent free entitlement
and then later be unexpectedly asked to pay.

Where participation terms depend upon eligibility, those terms should remain
pending until eligibility has been resolved.

Canonical sequence:

Account Created
        ↓
Workspace Available
        ↓
Eligibility Review
        ↓

COMMUNITY-SERVING
        ↓
commerce_status = not_required
        ↓
applicable entitlement enabled

COMMERCIAL
        ↓
commerce_status = required / pending
        ↓
package / commerce experience
        ↓
commerce_status = complete
        ↓
applicable entitlement enabled


================================================================================
NO RETROACTIVE PAYMENT SURPRISE PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

PurPaws should avoid the sequence:

participant appears fully approved
        ↓
participant publishes / receives applicable entitlement
        ↓
PurPaws later decides activity is commercial
        ↓
PurPaws asks participant to pay retroactively

Instead:

eligibility unresolved
        ↓
applicable entitlement remains pending
        ↓
classification completed
        ↓
correct participation terms are presented before entitlement is granted


================================================================================
12. WORKSPACE ENTITLEMENT
================================================================================

Account creation and workspace access may occur before eligibility review is
complete.

This allows participants to:

- complete their profile
- prepare content
- upload permitted draft materials
- continue onboarding
- save work
- review their information

Workspace access does not automatically equal publication entitlement.

Workspace access does not automatically equal commercial approval.

Workspace access does not automatically equal community eligibility.


================================================================================
13. PUBLICATION ENTITLEMENT
================================================================================

Publication is a separate entitlement.

Publication should eventually require the relevant conditions for the
specific canonical record being published.

Depending on record type, conditions may include:

- email verified
- canonical record exists
- required profile facts complete
- applicable eligibility resolved
- applicable commerce condition satisfied
- publication status approved / enabled

Not every PurPaws record must necessarily require organization eligibility.

For example, personal Companion or Trails activity may follow different
publication rules from a commercial Organization profile.

Publication rules must therefore be scoped to the applicable canonical record.


================================================================================
14. DIRECTORY PROJECTION
================================================================================

Directory is downstream from canonical publication.

Target sequence:

Participant / Entity / Record
        ↓
Relationships
        ↓
Classification
        ↓
Eligibility where applicable
        ↓
Commerce where applicable
        ↓
Publication
        ↓
Public Projection
        ↓
Directory Aggregation

Directory must not become the authority for deciding what the participant is.


================================================================================
CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION FACTS
================================================================================

The current onboarding implementation already provides:

- display_name
- email
- path / entry experience
- payload_json transitional storage
- email verification
- account creation
- Affiliate ID / referral information
- workspace draft state

The current implemented Directory intake provides:

- listing_name
- category
- location

The current frontend path payload architecture can collect additional
path-specific facts.

The existing organization model already contains:

- organization_name
- organization_type
- contact_name
- contact_email
- website_url
- location

and classification fields:

- declared_activity_nature
- activity_nature
- eligibility_status
- eligibility_basis
- eligibility_review_source
- eligibility_review_reference
- eligibility_review_notes
- eligibility_reviewed_at
- commerce_status

Those classification fields are presently structural and are not yet wired
into onboarding behavior.


================================================================================
CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION GAP
================================================================================

The current onboarding flow does not yet explicitly collect:

- acting capacity
- canonical participant nature
- organization branching
- declared_activity_nature
- eligibility evidence
- organization eligibility state
- commerce state

The current publish behavior also predates the canonical distinction between:

- email verification
- eligibility verification
- commerce completion

These gaps are implementation targets, not reasons to change the locked
ontology.


================================================================================
CANONICAL INTAKE FACT GROUPS
================================================================================

The first implementation of the canonical intake contract should therefore
work toward reusable fact groups rather than path-specific duplication.

Potential reusable fact groups include:

IDENTITY
- display_name
- email

CAPACITY
- acting_capacity

ORGANIZATION
- organization_name
- organization_type
- website_url
- location

ACTIVITY DECLARATION
- declared_activity_nature

EVIDENCE
- website_url
- future evidence references

INTENT
- onboarding path / requested experience

RELATIONSHIP
- relationship intent where applicable

These are semantic groups.

This document does not authorize exact frontend field names beyond those
already established in the existing model.


================================================================================
SHARED INTAKE VS PATH-SPECIFIC INTAKE
================================================================================

The future onboarding system should distinguish:

SHARED INTAKE

Facts useful across many paths.

Examples:

- identity
- acting capacity
- organization involvement
- declared activity nature where relevant
- website / evidence where relevant


PATH-SPECIFIC INTAKE

Facts needed only for the selected experience.

Examples:

Companion
- companion-specific facts

Memorial
- memorial-specific facts

Creator
- creator profile facts

Event
- event facts

Founder
- founder-specific facts

Partner
- collaboration intent / relationship facts


================================================================================
MODULARITY PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

Canonical intake must remain modular.

Shared intake concepts should not be hard-coded independently into every path.

Where practical, reusable modules should handle:

- identity
- organization
- classification declaration
- evidence
- eligibility status display
- commerce status display

Path-specific modules should handle only their own unique facts.

This supports:

- white-label architecture
- consistent validation
- consistent classification
- reduced duplication
- easier future path creation


================================================================================
WHITE-LABEL PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

The intake contract must not depend conceptually on PurPaws-specific frontend
page names.

The canonical layers should remain reusable for future branded deployments:

Identity
Capacity
Intent
Entity Facts
Relationships
Activity Declaration
Evidence
Verification
Canonicalization
Eligibility
Commerce
Workspace
Publication
Projection

Brand-specific copy and path labels may sit above this canonical contract.

IMPLEMENTATION ORDER — PROPOSED
================================================================================

This document does not authorize implementation yet.

After verification and lock, the recommended implementation sequence is:

1. Define exact canonical intake vocabulary.

2. Define acting_capacity values.

3. Define participant / organization branching.

4. Define declared_activity_nature values.

5. Define eligibility_status vocabulary.

6. Define commerce_status vocabulary.

7. Decide where transitional intake facts live before canonicalization.

8. Define eligibility review workflow.

9. Define entitlement gates.

10. Define shared reusable onboarding modules.

11. Introduce the contract into onboarding one controlled step at a time.

12. Verify legacy Directory flow remains operational during migration.

13. Canonicalize publication sources only after replacement flows exist.

14. Retire legacy Directory-as-identity behavior after verification.
