PURPAWS PARTNER PROJECTION SEMANTICS v1

STATUS

Defined for verification.

Not yet locked.


====================================================================================================
PURPOSE
====================================================================================================

Partner Projection Semantics v1 defines what "Partner" means inside the
PurPaws canonical network.

It separates:

Organization identity

from:

Partner relationship

from:

relationship basis

from:

relationship status

from:

Partner publication

from:

commerce.


====================================================================================================
CORE LAW
====================================================================================================

PARTNER IS NOT IDENTITY.

A Partner is not a distinct canonical entity type merely because an
organization participates in a Partner relationship.

The durable identity remains:

    Organization


Conceptually:

    Organization
        +
    explicit Partner relationship
        ->
    Partner projection eligibility


====================================================================================================
PARTNER DEFINITION
====================================================================================================

A PurPaws Partner is an Organization that has an explicit, recognized
relationship with the PurPaws network under a qualifying Partner basis.

Partner status must be supported by durable evidence.

Partner status must not be inferred from:

- Directory presence
- Organization existence
- organization name
- organization type
- website
- location
- event attendance
- FCIS research
- booth photographs
- contact history
- affiliate participation alone
- a purchase that does not establish a defined Partner relationship
- historical placement in the partner_organizations table alone


====================================================================================================
FIRST QUALIFYING BASES
====================================================================================================

Partner relationships may arise through multiple explicit bases.

v1 recognizes the semantic categories below.


1. FOUNDER MEMBERSHIP

An Organization holds a qualifying Founder membership that explicitly grants
or establishes Partner relationship status.

Conceptually:

    Organization
        ->
    Founder Membership
        ->
    Partner Relationship


Founder membership is one valid route to Partner status.

It is not the definition of Organization identity.


2. SPONSORSHIP

An Organization enters an explicit sponsorship relationship with PurPaws.

Examples may include:

- bank sponsor
- corporate sponsor
- event sponsor
- program sponsor
- campaign sponsor

Conceptually:

    Organization
        ->
    Sponsorship Agreement
        ->
    Partner Relationship


A bank sponsor therefore remains canonically:

    Organization

while its sponsorship establishes:

    Partner Relationship


3. COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIP

An Organization enters an explicit community collaboration with PurPaws.

Examples may include:

- rescue
- shelter
- community organization
- nonprofit
- animal-welfare group
- community venue

A commercial purchase is not required if the relationship is explicitly
recognized and authorized.


4. STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP

An Organization enters a strategic relationship with PurPaws.

Examples may include:

- financial institution
- technology provider
- media organization
- veterinary network
- venue
- institutional collaborator


5. CAMPAIGN PARTNERSHIP

An Organization participates as a Partner within a defined campaign.

Campaign partnership may be scoped.

It does not necessarily create permanent network-wide Partner status.

Conceptually:

    Organization
        ->
    Partner Relationship
        basis = campaign
        scope = campaign
        ->
    Partner Projection within that scope


6. NETWORK PARTNERSHIP

An Organization enters an explicit broader PurPaws network relationship under
an agreement or recognized network-participation basis.


====================================================================================================
RELATIONSHIP BASIS
====================================================================================================

The relationship basis answers:

    WHY is this Organization a Partner?


Example canonical vocabulary may include:

    founder_membership
    sponsorship
    community_partnership
    strategic_partnership
    campaign_partnership
    network_partnership


This vocabulary describes the basis of a relationship.

It does not redefine Organization identity.


====================================================================================================
RELATIONSHIP STATUS
====================================================================================================

Partner relationship status answers:

    IS the Partner relationship currently in force?


Conceptual states may include:

    pending
    active
    inactive
    expired
    suspended
    ended


Exact executable vocabulary requires a separate implementation contract.

Relationship status must remain separate from:

- Organization operational status
- canonical publication status
- eligibility status
- commerce status


====================================================================================================
RELATIONSHIP SCOPE
====================================================================================================

A Partner relationship may have scope.

Examples:

    network
    campaign
    event
    program
    project


Scope matters.

A campaign-scoped Partner relationship must not silently become a permanent
network-wide Partner relationship.


====================================================================================================
FOUNDER MEMBERSHIP
====================================================================================================

Founder membership may establish Partner status when the Founder membership
definition explicitly grants a Partner relationship.

The correct semantic chain is:

    Organization
        ->
    Founder Membership
        ->
    Partner Relationship


Not:

    payment
        ->
    Partner identity


====================================================================================================
SPONSORSHIP
====================================================================================================

Sponsorship is a first-class qualifying basis for Partner relationship.

Example:

    Bank
        ->
    canonical Organization
        ->
    explicit sponsorship agreement
        ->
    Partner relationship
        ->
    Partner projection


The bank remains an Organization.

"Partner" describes how the bank is connected to PurPaws.


====================================================================================================
COMMERCE SEPARATION
====================================================================================================

COMMERCE != PARTNER RELATIONSHIP.

A payment may provide evidence that a qualifying membership, sponsorship, or
agreement was purchased.

The transaction itself does not become the semantic relationship.


Conceptually:

    Payment / Commerce Event
        ->
    satisfies or activates
        ->
    qualifying relationship basis
        ->
    Partner Relationship


Therefore:

payment status

and:

Partner relationship status

must remain separate durable questions.


====================================================================================================
PUBLICATION SEPARATION
====================================================================================================

PARTNER RELATIONSHIP != PARTNER PUBLICATION.

An Organization may hold an active Partner relationship without necessarily
having a published Partner projection.

Partner publication requires explicit publication authority.

Therefore:

    Organization existence
        !=
    Partner relationship

    Partner relationship
        !=
    Partner projection publication


====================================================================================================
ORGANIZATION PUBLICATION INDEPENDENCE
====================================================================================================

Organization publication and Partner publication answer different questions.

An Organization may be:

    canonically published as an Organization

without:

    being a Partner.


An Organization may hold:

    an active Partner relationship

while Partner projection publication remains separately controlled.


====================================================================================================
MULTI-PROJECTION COEXISTENCE
====================================================================================================

One canonical Organization may legitimately support multiple public
projections.

Example:

    organization:2
        ->
    Organization projection

and:

    organization:2
        ->
    Partner relationship
        ->
    Partner projection


These projections may coexist when their public semantics are intentionally
different.

They must not be collapsed merely because they share one canonical
Organization identity.


====================================================================================================
CURRENT LEGACY PartnerOrganization BOUNDARY
====================================================================================================

The existing PartnerOrganization / partner_organizations structure is a legacy
application structure.

Its existence does not by itself establish canonical Partner relationship
authority.

A row in:

    partner_organizations

does NOT automatically prove:

    founder membership
    sponsorship
    community partnership
    strategic partnership
    campaign partnership
    network partnership


The historical table may continue to support transitional application
behavior while canonical Partner relationship semantics are implemented
separately.


====================================================================================================
CURRENT partner-2 RULE
====================================================================================================

partner-2 currently exists as a legacy public Partner projection.

Partner Projection Semantics v1 does NOT retroactively invent a relationship
basis for partner-2.

Until explicit evidence or a future migration establishes a qualifying Partner
relationship:

    partner-2 relationship basis = unresolved / legacy

This design does not remove partner-2.

This design does not declare partner-2 canonically qualified.

This design preserves the current projection while relationship authority is
designed.


====================================================================================================
CURRENT partner-1 RULE
====================================================================================================

partner-1 is subject to the same principle.

Historical presence in partner_organizations does not itself establish a
canonical Partner relationship basis.

No Founder, sponsor, strategic, campaign, or other Partner basis may be
invented without evidence.


====================================================================================================
PARTNER PROJECTION ELIGIBILITY
====================================================================================================

Conceptually, future Partner projection eligibility requires:

1. canonical Organization identity;

2. explicit Partner relationship;

3. qualifying relationship basis;

4. relationship status that permits projection;

5. explicit Partner publication authority.


Conceptually:

    Organization
        +
    Partner Relationship
        +
    Qualifying Basis
        +
    Eligible Relationship Status
        +
    Partner Publication
        ->
    Public Partner Projection


====================================================================================================
NO INFERENCE
====================================================================================================

Partner status must never be inferred from:

- category
- organization_type
- organization name
- domain
- website
- matching email
- payment amount alone
- historical table placement
- campaign existence alone
- Directory publication
- canonical Organization publication
- affiliate enrollment
- FCIS observation alone


====================================================================================================
FCIS
====================================================================================================

FCIS may collect evidence suggesting a potential Partner relationship.

Examples:

- sponsorship conversation
- Founder membership purchase
- signed agreement
- campaign agreement
- collaboration commitment
- explicit community partnership

FCIS evidence may support relationship determination.

FCIS observation itself does not automatically create the relationship.


====================================================================================================
WHITE-LABEL PRINCIPLE
====================================================================================================

This architecture is not PurPaws-specific at the identity layer.

A white-label deployment may use other relationship labels while preserving
the same pattern:

    canonical entity
        +
    explicit relationship
        +
    relationship basis
        +
    relationship status
        +
    publication authority
        ->
    relationship projection

FIRST FUTURE IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT
====================================================================================================

Before canonical Partner projection execution begins, a separate implementation
contract must define durable Partner relationship authority.

That future contract must determine at minimum:

- relationship target Organization
- relationship type
- relationship basis
- relationship status
- relationship scope
- evidence / provenance
- effective dates where required
- publication separation
- idempotency
- correction semantics


====================================================================================================
SUCCESS CONDITIONS
====================================================================================================

Partner Projection Semantics v1 succeeds if it preserves all of the following:

1. Partner is not identity.

2. Canonical Organization remains identity authority.

3. Founder membership may establish Partner relationship.

4. Sponsorship may establish Partner relationship.

5. Community partnership may establish Partner relationship.

6. Strategic partnership may establish Partner relationship.

7. Campaign partnership may be scoped.

8. Network partnership may be explicit.

9. Commerce remains separate from relationship semantics.

10. Publication remains separate from relationship semantics.

11. Historical PartnerOrganization rows do not automatically become canonical
    Partner relationships.

12. partner-1 and partner-2 receive no invented relationship basis.

13. Different legitimate projections may coexist.

14. FCIS evidence may support but does not silently create relationship truth.
