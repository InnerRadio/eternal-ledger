FOUNDATIONAL BOUNDARIES

Attribution may precede membership.

Campaign Participation requires membership.

Reward may be determined from attributable activity.

Settlement requires membership.

Participant-specific settlement requires a resolved PurPaws member.

Membership establishes WHO may receive value.

Campaign Reward Policy determines WHAT that activity earns.

REFERRER / PARTICIPANT

Referrer identity remains distinct from participant identity.

Referring Affiliate ID remains distinct from participant Affiliate ID.

Participant resolution must not overwrite historical referrer provenance.

CAMPAIGN

campaign_id uses the existing semantic String campaign identity.

Campaign attribution must not later be reconstructed from Affiliate ID alone.

EVIDENCE

Evidence remains generic.

Click is optional evidence.

Attribution Context is not an AffiliateClick wrapper.

CONVERSION

AttributionContext does not contain a singular conversion_id.

One Attribution Context may eventually support zero, one, or multiple Conversions.

Future Conversion linkage is expected to point from Conversion toward
the proven Attribution Context, subject to later implementation inspection.

REWARD NEUTRALITY

AttributionContext contains no payout, commission, reward, token,
settlement, or payment fields.

NULLABILITY

Participant and Enrollment references may begin NULL intentionally.

This represents genuine pre-membership lifecycle state.

FOREIGN KEY STYLE

No explicit SQLAlchemy ForeignKey declarations are introduced in this
schema contract.

Existing repository persistence convention is preserved.
