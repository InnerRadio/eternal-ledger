FOUNDATIONAL AUTHORITY

Attribution Context may exist BEFORE membership.

Campaign Participation requires an identified member.

Referrer identity != participant identity.

Referring Affiliate ID != participant Affiliate ID.

Original Campaign provenance survives membership resolution.

Original referrer provenance survives membership resolution.

Click evidence != Campaign Participation.

Campaign Participation != Attribution Context.

Conversion != Attribution Context.

Attribution Context != Reward Policy.

Attribution Context remains reward-neutral.

Context must be captured before or during the attributed journey.

Transport mechanism != attribution authority.

LIFECYCLE

ANONYMOUS / INBOUND
    ↓
IDENTIFIED
    ↓
PARTICIPANT
    ↓
CONVERTED
    ↓
REWARD-CONSUMABLE

EXAMPLE

Member A shares Campaign X.

Person B arrives through A.

Attribution preserves:
- Campaign X
- A as referrer
- A Affiliate identity
- inbound evidence

B then registers.

B receives:
- B user_id
- B Affiliate ID
- B referral code

If B joins Campaign X:
- Campaign Participation belongs to B
- original attribution to A remains preserved

CURRENT EXISTING RECORDS

AffiliateClick
= inbound evidence

AffiliateCampaignEnrollment
= Campaign Participation

AffiliateConversion
= qualifying event

AffiliateCommission
= downstream cash-specific economic record

None of these records is the entire Attribution Context authority.

FIRST-CLASS AUTHORITY

A first-class Attribution Context is justified because no existing
record can preserve all of:

- pre-membership origin
- Campaign identity
- referrer identity
- inbound evidence
- later member identity
- later Campaign Participation identity
- subsequent Conversion lineage

without changing the meaning of that existing record.
