PURPAWS — TAXONOMY GOVERNANCE v1

STATUS
LOCKED ARCHITECTURAL AUTHORITY CONTRACT

PURPOSE

This contract defines the authority boundary for PurPaws taxonomy and
classification.

Taxonomy is part of the platform-curated intelligence layer.

The presence of an organization, campaign, participant, relationship,
membership, publication, or other entity in a taxonomy assignment does
not transfer ownership of taxonomy authority to that entity or to a
person who has operational authority over that entity.


CORE AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

Platform-curated intelligence
!= Organization access
!= Campaign authority
!= Membership
!= Relationship
!= Publication


TAXONOMY AUTHORITY

PartnerTaxonomyCategory represents platform-defined taxonomy vocabulary.

PartnerTaxonomyAssignment represents the application of platform taxonomy
to an entity or governed context.

Taxonomy authority is presently a platform administrative authority.

Current taxonomy category and assignment management remains restricted
to platform administrative authority.


ORGANIZATION ACCESS SEPARATION

Organization access does not imply taxonomy authority.

The following capabilities do not, by themselves, authorize taxonomy
creation, taxonomy modification, taxonomy assignment, taxonomy removal,
or taxonomy administration:

- view_organization
- edit_organization
- manage_campaigns
- administer_members

OrganizationMember existence does not confer taxonomy authority.

Organization membership role does not confer taxonomy authority.


CAMPAIGN AUTHORITY SEPARATION

Campaign management does not imply taxonomy authority.

A taxonomy assignment may reference a campaign without transferring
classification authority to a campaign manager.

Campaign operators may operate campaigns according to their separately
governed capabilities.

They do not thereby gain authority to alter PurPaws platform
classification intelligence.


RELATIONSHIP SEPARATION

CanonicalRelationship does not confer taxonomy authority.

A recognized relationship between a participant and an organization
does not authorize taxonomy mutation.

RELATIONSHIP != TAXONOMY AUTHORITY


MEMBERSHIP SEPARATION

OrganizationMember does not confer taxonomy authority merely through
membership existence or role.

MEMBERSHIP != TAXONOMY AUTHORITY


PUBLICATION SEPARATION

CanonicalPublication does not confer taxonomy authority.

Publication eligibility, publication state, or public visibility does
not authorize mutation of platform taxonomy.

PUBLICATION != TAXONOMY AUTHORITY


CANONICAL IDENTITY SEPARATION

Canonicalization provenance establishes source-to-canonical identity.

It does not establish taxonomy authority.

Organization-resolution review establishes an identity judgment.

It does not establish taxonomy authority.

IDENTITY != TAXONOMY AUTHORITY


CURRENT RUNTIME CLASSIFICATION

The currently inspected taxonomy route family is platform-administered.

Taxonomy category management is admin / super_admin controlled.

Taxonomy assignment management is admin / super_admin controlled.

No organization self-management semantics were established by the
inspected runtime.

No public taxonomy management route was established by the inspected
runtime.

Therefore no Organization Access Authority capability is added for
taxonomy in v1.


IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

An entity may be the SUBJECT of classification without being the
AUTHORITY over classification.

An organization may have taxonomy assignments.

A campaign may have taxonomy assignments.

Neither fact means that organization operators or campaign operators
control PurPaws taxonomy.


FUTURE CHANGE RULE

If PurPaws later introduces organization self-classification, campaign
self-classification, public taxonomy editing, suggested classifications,
or another non-admin taxonomy workflow, that capability must be designed
as an explicit authority contract.

It must not be inferred from:

- organization membership
- organization access
- campaign management
- canonical relationship
- canonical identity
- publication
- provenance

Any such future authority requires:

Observe
→ Inspect
→ Understand
→ Replace / Add
→ Verify
→ Lock
→ Commit
→ Push
→ Continue


v1 GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Taxonomy is platform-curated intelligence.

Platform-curated intelligence
!= Organization access
!= Campaign authority
!= Membership
!= Relationship
!= Publication

No authority inheritance is implicit.
