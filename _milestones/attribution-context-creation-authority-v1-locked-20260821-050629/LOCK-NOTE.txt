GOVERNING SECURITY PRINCIPLE

CLIENT INPUT IS NOT CANONICAL ATTRIBUTION TRUTH.

The client may present evidence.

The server decides what that evidence proves.

Canonical attribution is server-resolved.

TRUST BOUNDARY

Client-supplied values may be:

- claims
- references
- evidence
- transport

They do not automatically establish:

- Campaign authority
- referrer authority
- participant identity
- Campaign Participation
- Conversion
- Reward
- Commission
- Token
- Settlement

ACTOR SEPARATION

System actor != Referrer != Participant

ANONYMOUS STATE

Anonymous Attribution Context:
PERMITTED

Anonymous Campaign Participation:
NOT CANONICAL

Anonymous settlement:
PROHIBITED

Participant-specific settlement requires membership.

SPOOF RESISTANCE

Client participant identity trusted:
NO

Client enrollment identity trusted:
NO

Client reward / payout value trusted:
NO

Client Campaign claim canonical by itself:
NO

Client referrer claim canonical by itself:
NO

DUPLICATE / REPLAY

Explicit duplicate / idempotency policy is REQUIRED before creation
implementation.

Replay resistance is REQUIRED before reusable attribution references
become authoritative.

HISTORICAL PROVENANCE

Later lifecycle resolution may ADD trusted knowledge.

It must not falsify or overwrite historical origin.

SAFE FAILURE

If provenance cannot be established:

DO NOT INVENT IT.

ADMINISTRATION

Administrative privilege does not eliminate provenance requirements.
