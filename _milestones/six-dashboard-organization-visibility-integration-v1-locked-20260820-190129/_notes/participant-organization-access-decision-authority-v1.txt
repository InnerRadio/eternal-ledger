PURPAWS / ETERNAL LEDGER
PARTICIPANT ↔ ORGANIZATION ACCESS DECISION AUTHORITY v1

STATUS
LOCKED ARCHITECTURAL CONTRACT

PURPOSE

Define the authority boundary that determines whether a Participant with a
recognized canonical relationship to an Organization may receive operational
access to that Organization inside PurPaws / Eternal Ledger.

CORE PRINCIPLE

RELATIONSHIP != ACCESS

A recognized canonical relationship is relevant evidence.

It is not itself an access grant.

THREE DISTINCT QUESTIONS

CanonicalRelationship answers:

    "What relationship do we recognize between these subjects?"

AccessDecision answers:

    "Should this account receive operational authority for this Organization?"

OrganizationMember answers:

    "What operational access is currently enforced by the application?"

These concepts must remain separate.

ACCESS DECISION VOCABULARY

GRANT

    Operational access is explicitly approved.

DENY

    Operational access is explicitly denied.

DEFER

    Available evidence is insufficient to safely grant or deny access.

REVOKE

    Previously granted operational access is explicitly withdrawn.

DEFER RATHER THAN GUESS.

ACCESS DECISION INPUTS

A future executable Access Decision Authority must receive explicit inputs
including:

    participant user identity
    canonical organization identity
    supporting CanonicalRelationship
    decision
    decision basis
    evidence summary
    requested or approved operational role when applicable
    reviewer / authority identity
    decision timestamp

A GRANT must never be produced merely because a CanonicalRelationship exists.

RELATIONSHIP REQUIREMENT

A canonical Participant → Organization relationship may be required before
access consideration.

The relationship may provide evidence that the Participant is genuinely
associated with the Organization.

That relationship alone is insufficient to grant operational authority.

CURRENT CONTROLLED CASE

Participant:

    user:8

Organization:

    organization:2

Canonical relationship:

    relationship_type = representative
    relationship_basis = verified_onboarding_submission
    relationship_status = active
    evidence = onboarding_record:15

Current operational access state:

    OrganizationMember user:8 → organization:2 = NONE

Therefore:

    User #8 has a recognized relationship to Organization #2.

But:

    User #8 has not yet been granted operational access to Organization #2.

This is correct.

FIRST SUBMITTER RULE

The first Participant to submit verified Organization information does NOT
automatically become:

    owner
    steward
    administrator
    manager
    publisher
    teammate inviter
    workspace controller

Submission sequence is not authority.

Evidence of relationship is not evidence of ownership.

MULTI-PARTICIPANT RULE

Multiple Participants may legitimately have canonical relationships to the
same Organization.

Each Participant must receive an independent access decision.

Example:

    user:A -> representative -> organization:X
    user:B -> representative -> organization:X

does not imply:

    user:A access = user:B access

Each access decision stands on its own evidence and authority.

MULTI-LOCATION RULE

Operational access to an Organization must not silently imply operational
authority over every physical Location belonging to that Organization.

Organization authority and Location authority may require separate scopes in
future versions.

The v1 architecture must preserve that distinction.

ORGANIZATION MEMBER RULE

OrganizationMember is the current application enforcement record for
Organization-specific operational access.

An OrganizationMember record may be created only after explicit access
authority has approved the grant.

OrganizationMember must not be created automatically by:

    onboarding creation
    email verification
    account creation
    canonical organization resolution
    canonicalization provenance
    canonical relationship creation
    public publication
    Directory discovery

ACCESS ROLE BOUNDARY

A future GRANT must include an explicit operational role.

Operational role vocabulary is NOT defined by this v1 authority contract.

Do not infer an operational role from the canonical relationship type.

In particular:

    representative != owner
    representative != manager
    representative != administrator
    representative != steward

ROLE VOCABULARY MUST BE LOCKED SEPARATELY.

CURRENT APPLICATION WARNING

Existing application behavior uses active OrganizationMember records as an
access signal.

Therefore an OrganizationMember row is not a harmless descriptive record.

Creating an active OrganizationMember may enable real application capability.

For this reason:

    DO NOT CREATE ORGANIZATION MEMBERSHIP BEFORE ACCESS AUTHORITY IS ESTABLISHED.

HISTORY PRINCIPLE

Access decisions are historically valuable.

Future durable Access Decision records should preserve decision history rather
than silently overwrite prior judgments.

Historical decision != current-effective access state.

A future durable implementation should support append-only access decision
history and derive current-effective authority from that history.

REVOCATION PRINCIPLE

Operational access may be revoked without deleting or falsifying the historical
canonical relationship.

For example:

    canonical relationship remains:
        representative

while:

    access decision becomes:
        REVOKE

and:

    OrganizationMember becomes inactive or otherwise loses effective access.

Truth history and access state therefore remain independently governable.

TEAMMATE INVITATION BOUNDARY

Teammate invitation is separate authority.

Access to an Organization does not automatically imply permission to grant
access to other Participants.

Future teammate invitation authority must explicitly define:

    who may invite
    whom they may invite
    what Organization or Location scope applies
    what role may be granted
    whether acceptance is required
    how invitations expire or are revoked

INVITATION AUTHORITY != ACCESS AUTHORITY.

OWNERSHIP / STEWARDSHIP BOUNDARY

Ownership, stewardship, executive authority, employment, and administration
are real-world relationship claims.

They require evidence appropriate to those claims.

Operational access must not silently rewrite canonical relationship truth.

Likewise canonical relationship truth must not silently grant operational
access.

CURRENT CONTROLLED DECISION

For:

    user:8
        ->
    organization:2

the current access state is:

    NO ACCESS DECISION YET

Therefore:

    OrganizationMember remains absent.

No GRANT is inferred by this architecture lock.

NO ACCESS MUTATION IS AUTHORIZED BY THIS NOTE.

IMPLEMENTATION SEQUENCE

1. Lock Access Decision Authority architecture.
2. Inspect operational role vocabulary and current enforcement semantics.
3. Define and lock launch-safe operational role vocabulary.
4. Define durable append-only Access Decision history.
5. Implement executable Access Decision Authority.
6. Dry-run the controlled user:8 -> organization:2 case.
7. Lock executable authority.
8. Create an explicit access decision only after review.
9. Only then create or modify OrganizationMember if the effective decision
   authorizes it.
10. Verify application capability separately.

LOCKED INVARIANTS

CanonicalRelationship != AccessDecision.

AccessDecision != OrganizationMember.

Canonical relationship existence does not imply access.

First submitter does not automatically receive privileged authority.

Multiple Participants may relate to one Organization.

Each Participant receives an independent access decision.

Operational role must be explicit.

Operational role must not be inferred from relationship_type.

OrganizationMember is an enforcement record, not canonical relationship truth.

Access can be revoked without deleting historical relationship truth.

Teammate invitation is separate authority.

DEFER RATHER THAN GUESS.

RELATIONSHIP != PERMISSION.
