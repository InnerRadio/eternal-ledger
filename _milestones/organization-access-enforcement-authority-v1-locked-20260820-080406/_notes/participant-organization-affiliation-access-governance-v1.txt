PURPAWS / ETERNAL LEDGER
PARTICIPANT ↔ ORGANIZATION AFFILIATION + ACCESS GOVERNANCE v1

STATUS
LOCKED ARCHITECTURAL CONTRACT

PURPOSE

Define the boundary between:

1. evidence,
2. canonical identity resolution,
3. canonical relationships,
4. operational organization membership,
5. permissions and workspace authority.

CORE PRINCIPLE

A relationship is not a permission.

Evidence that a Participant is associated with an Organization may justify
recognition of a canonical relationship.

It does not, by itself, authorize that Participant to control, edit, publish,
administer, or otherwise act on behalf of that Organization.

ARCHITECTURAL FLOW

Evidence
    ↓
Canonical Organization Resolution
    ↓
Canonical Participant ↔ Organization Relationship
    ↓
Explicit Access Decision
    ↓
OrganizationMember
    ↓
Workspace / Dashboard Authority

LAYER 1 — EVIDENCE

Evidence records what was observed, submitted, verified, reviewed, or otherwise
established.

Evidence may support identity resolution or relationship recognition.

Evidence does not itself grant operational authority.

LAYER 2 — CANONICAL ORGANIZATION RESOLUTION

Canonical organization resolution determines which canonical Organization an
incoming source record refers to.

Resolution answers:

    "Which Organization is this?"

Resolution does not answer:

    "Who may control this Organization?"

Resolution must not create membership or permission.

LAYER 3 — CANONICAL RELATIONSHIP

CanonicalRelationship represents an evidence-backed relationship between
canonical subjects.

Examples may include:

    Participant → Organization
    Organization → Organization
    Organization → Campaign
    Participant → Campaign

A canonical relationship contains explicit semantics including:

    source
    target
    relationship_type
    relationship_basis
    relationship_status
    scope
    evidence

CanonicalRelationship belongs to the intelligence / truth layer.

It is not an access-control record.

LAYER 4 — ACCESS DECISION

Operational authority requires a separate explicit decision.

The system must determine whether the recognized relationship is sufficient
to grant access and, if so, what access is appropriate.

The decision must not be inferred merely from:

    matching email addresses
    onboarding ownership
    canonicalization provenance
    organization resolution
    contact information
    public profile information
    relationship existence

The access decision must be explicit and auditable.

LAYER 5 — ORGANIZATION MEMBER

OrganizationMember belongs to the operational authority layer.

It connects:

    user_id
    organization_id
    role
    status

An active OrganizationMember record may enable organization-specific account,
dashboard, workspace, campaign, or management functionality according to the
existing account authority.

OrganizationMember must not be silently created by canonical resolution.

OrganizationMember must not be silently created merely because a canonical
relationship exists.

LAYER 6 — WORKSPACE / DASHBOARD AUTHORITY

Workspace and dashboard authority are downstream consequences of approved
operational membership and account authority.

The UI must consume authority.

The UI must not invent authority.

GOVERNANCE INVARIANTS

INVARIANT 1

Canonical identity resolution and operational access are separate concerns.

INVARIANT 2

CanonicalRelationship and OrganizationMember are separate records with
separate responsibilities.

INVARIANT 3

CanonicalRelationship records recognized truth.

OrganizationMember records operational authority.

INVARIANT 4

Relationship recognition must not automatically grant permission.

INVARIANT 5

Organization resolution must not automatically create OrganizationMember.

INVARIANT 6

A participant may have a legitimate canonical relationship to an Organization
without having administrative or publishing authority over that Organization.

INVARIANT 7

Operational authority must be explicit, reviewable, and revocable.

INVARIANT 8

Access roles must describe operational authority rather than merely describing
the real-world relationship.

INVARIANT 9

Evidence supporting relationship recognition must remain distinguishable from
the decision granting operational access.

INVARIANT 10

Public discovery, publication, canonical intelligence, communication permission,
and organization operational authority remain separate governance domains.

CURRENT CONTROLLED CASE

Participant:

    User #8

Canonical Organization:

    Organization #2
    PurPaws Testing

Current inspected state:

    User #8 exists.
    Organization #2 exists.
    Organization #2 is canonical.
    No direct User #8 ↔ Organization #2 OrganizationMember link exists.

Therefore:

    No OrganizationMember record is to be inferred merely from canonical
    organization resolution.

The next controlled operation may establish an evidence-backed canonical
Participant ↔ Organization relationship.

Only after that relationship is understood and verified should a separate
operational access decision be considered.

DO NOT COLLAPSE

Do not collapse:

    Evidence
    CanonicalizationProvenance
    CanonicalRelationship
    OrganizationMember
    CommunicationPermission
    Publication authority
    Workspace authority

into one concept.

They answer different questions.

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

CanonicalizationProvenance:

    "Where did this canonical identity resolution come from?"

CanonicalRelationship:

    "What recognized relationship exists between these canonical subjects?"

OrganizationMember:

    "What operational role does this account have inside this Organization?"

CommunicationPermission:

    "What communications are permitted?"

Publication authority:

    "What may be published, and under whose authority?"

Workspace authority:

    "What operational interfaces and actions may this account access?"

LOCK

This governance contract must be preserved as Participant ↔ Organization
affiliation and access functionality evolves.

Future implementations may extend roles, review workflows, invitations,
verification, stewardship, ownership transfer, and delegated authority.

They must preserve the separation:

    RELATIONSHIP ≠ PERMISSION

END
