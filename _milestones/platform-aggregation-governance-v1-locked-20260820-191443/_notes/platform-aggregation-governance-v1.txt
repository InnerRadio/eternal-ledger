PURPAWS — PLATFORM AGGREGATION GOVERNANCE v1

STATUS
GOVERNING ARCHITECTURE

PURPOSE

Define the authority boundary for platform-wide aggregation systems,
including Platform Leaderboard and Network Pulse.

This contract prevents platform intelligence from being incorrectly
collapsed into organization access authority.

======================================================================
CORE SEPARATION
======================================================================

Platform Aggregation
!= Organization Access
!= Campaign Authority
!= Membership
!= Relationship
!= Publication
!= Taxonomy Authority

Aggregation Truth
!= Public Projection

======================================================================
1. PLATFORM AGGREGATION
======================================================================

Platform aggregation answers questions about the network as a whole.

Examples:

- How large is the community?
- How much activity exists across the network?
- Which participants or organizations rank according to defined metrics?
- What patterns, counts, scores, and signals exist across the platform?
- What public-safe community heartbeat should be surfaced?

Platform aggregation may consume records from multiple canonical and
operational domains as source data.

The presence of organization, membership, campaign, relationship,
publication, or taxonomy data inside an aggregate does not transfer
authority from those domains to the aggregation system.

DATA INPUT != AUTHORITY GRANT.

======================================================================
2. PLATFORM LEADERBOARD
======================================================================

Platform Leaderboard is a platform/community intelligence aggregator.

Its purpose is to calculate comparative network rankings and reputation
signals across participants and organizations.

OrganizationMember may participate as source evidence for ranking
components.

OrganizationMember is NOT an authorization gate for the leaderboard.

The leaderboard MUST NOT be converted into a "my organizations" feature
by filtering its platform-wide inputs through dashboard organization
visibility.

Therefore:

get_visible_organizations()
MUST NOT define the leaderboard population.

view_organization
MUST NOT define the leaderboard population.

Organization access roles
MUST NOT determine whether an otherwise eligible platform entity exists
inside platform-wide ranking calculations.

======================================================================
3. NETWORK PULSE
======================================================================

Network Pulse is the public-facing community heartbeat aggregator.

It describes network-scale state and activity.

Examples include:

- participant counts
- organization counts
- creator counts
- rescue counts
- affiliate counts
- memorial counts
- contribution counts
- media activity
- active public opportunity/campaign counts
- future public-safe leaderboard highlights

Network Pulse is NOT private organization analytics.

Network Pulse is NOT a participant-specific organization dashboard.

Organization access authority MUST NOT determine the population of
community-wide Pulse metrics.

======================================================================
4. AGGREGATION TRUTH
======================================================================

Aggregation Truth is the internal evidence-backed analytical result.

It may use data that is legitimate for internal computation but is not
appropriate for unrestricted public exposure.

Examples may include:

- internal user identifiers
- internal organization identifiers
- account roles
- membership relationships
- referral identifiers
- analytical component values
- internal scoring inputs
- other operational identifiers

The fact that data may be used to calculate an aggregate does NOT mean
that data may be publicly exposed.

ANALYTICAL INPUT != PUBLIC FIELD.

======================================================================
5. PUBLIC AGGREGATION PROJECTION
======================================================================

Any platform aggregation promoted to a public surface MUST pass through
a public-safe projection boundary.

Public-safe projection may expose only fields intentionally approved for
public/community discovery.

Examples may include:

- approved public display identity
- public organization identity
- rank
- score where appropriate
- aggregate counts
- public badges/status
- public-safe metric components
- approved public profile references

Public projection MUST NOT automatically expose:

- private email addresses
- private phone numbers
- account credentials
- authentication information
- private referral identifiers
- internal-only account identifiers where unnecessary
- private membership administration data
- organization permissions
- unpublished operational records
- private commerce data

PUBLIC PROJECTION MUST BE EXPLICIT.

======================================================================
6. LEADERBOARD PUBLICATION BOUNDARY
======================================================================

The current Platform Leaderboard may contain fields appropriate for an
authenticated/internal analytical response that are not automatically
approved for public promotion.

Therefore:

Platform Leaderboard
        ->
Aggregation Truth
        ->
Public-Safe Projection
        ->
Network Pulse / Public Discovery

NOT:

Platform Leaderboard
        ->
raw public exposure

Any future promotion of leaderboard highlights into Network Pulse MUST
use an explicit public-safe projection.

======================================================================
7. MEMBERSHIP SEMANTICS
======================================================================

Membership can be evidence inside aggregation.

Membership does not become aggregation authority.

For example:

An active OrganizationMember row may contribute to a count, score,
relationship signal, or ranking component.

That does NOT mean:

- the viewer receives organization access;
- the member receives campaign authority;
- the member receives publication authority;
- the member receives taxonomy authority;
- the leaderboard becomes membership-scoped;
- the Network Pulse becomes membership-scoped.

MEMBERSHIP AS DATA != MEMBERSHIP AS PERMISSION.

======================================================================
8. RELATIONSHIP SEMANTICS
======================================================================

CanonicalRelationship may become an analytical input to future
aggregation systems.

CanonicalRelationship remains intelligence/truth.

CanonicalRelationship does not grant permission.

RELATIONSHIP AS SIGNAL != RELATIONSHIP AS PERMISSION.

======================================================================
9. TAXONOMY SEMANTICS
======================================================================

Platform-curated taxonomy may become an input to aggregation,
segmentation, discovery, ranking, or community intelligence.

Taxonomy remains platform-curated intelligence.

Taxonomy does not become organization access.

TAXONOMY SIGNAL != ORGANIZATION AUTHORITY.

======================================================================
10. CAMPAIGN SEMANTICS
======================================================================

Campaign activity may contribute to aggregate scores and community
activity metrics.

Campaign data appearing inside an aggregate does not grant campaign
management authority.

CAMPAIGN METRIC != MANAGE_CAMPAIGNS.

======================================================================
11. PUBLICATION SEMANTICS
======================================================================

Published records may contribute to public aggregation.

Aggregation does not itself publish canonical records.

AGGREGATION != PUBLICATION AUTHORITY.

======================================================================
12. AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES
======================================================================

Organization Access Authority answers:

"What may this participant do with this organization?"

Platform Aggregation answers:

"What does the network evidence tell us?"

Public Aggregation Projection answers:

"What portion of that aggregate intelligence is safe and appropriate
to expose on this public surface?"

These are three separate responsibilities.

======================================================================

======================================================================

Classification:
PLATFORM / COMMUNITY AGGREGATOR

Organization membership:
SOURCE DATA / RANKING EVIDENCE

NOT POPULATION AUTHORITY

Public-safe projection:
REQUIRED BEFORE RAW LEADERBOARD RECORDS ARE PROMOTED TO PUBLIC SURFACES


/account/network-pulse

Classification:
PUBLIC COMMUNITY AGGREGATOR

Purpose:
PUBLIC-SAFE COMMUNITY HEARTBEAT

Organization access authority:
NOT POPULATION AUTHORITY

Leaderboard promotion:
MUST USE PUBLIC-SAFE PROJECTION

======================================================================
14. NON-GRANTS
======================================================================

This governance contract does NOT grant:

- organization access
- organization editing
- campaign management
- member administration
- publication authority
- taxonomy authority
- commerce authority
- communication permission
- ownership
- stewardship
- teammate invitation authority

======================================================================
15. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

Platform Aggregation
!= Organization Access
!= Campaign Authority
!= Membership
!= Relationship
!= Publication
!= Taxonomy Authority

Aggregation Truth
!= Public Projection

Data may inform intelligence without transferring authority.

Public exposure must be an explicit projection decision.
